## Market Structure

### Two types of competitors

Laboratory companies (e.g. Novogenia): primary competence in laboratory analytics, own labs, deep scientific expertise, optimal quality control. Weakness: often less marketing-focused. Marketing companies: strong brand recognition. Weakness: superficial lab science, the laboratory is just a supplier, harder to control quality.

### Where Novogenia sits

Novogenia is a laboratory-led company that has built out a marketing capability — combining lab depth with the partner-network reach typically associated with marketing-led brands. This is the structural advantage to lead with.

## How to Position vs Specific Competitor Types

### Versus mass-market DNA test brands

These competitors typically offer one-page summaries and standardised dosing rounded to pre-formulated products. Lead with: deeper science (≥3 studies per gene, PubMed citations), milligram-precision personalisation, and own laboratory rather than outsourced.

### Versus boutique nutrition coaches without testing

These competitors rely on questionnaires and intuition. Lead with: genetics-confirmed traits remove guesswork; the analysis identifies blockers (MTHFR, NQO1, VDR) the coach cannot detect by interview.

### Versus pharmacies without personalisation

These competitors sell standardised supplements at scale. Lead with: personalisation avoids the statistical problem (1 in 20 nutrients ineffective for any given person, 1 in 20 potentially harmful, the rest mis-dosed) and the {{PELLET_NAME}} delivery technology that capsules cannot match.
