## Overview

### What is {{NUTRITION_SENSOR}}?

{{NUTRITION_SENSOR}} is a genetic analysis that examines how an individual's DNA interacts with diet. Over 50 genes are analysed across roughly 20 metabolic risk areas, producing a 200+ page personalised report, a personalised food rating across approximately 900–1,400 foods, and a personalised micronutrient recipe.

### Who is {{NUTRITION_SENSOR}} for?

People interested in healthy eating, in optimising supplementation, with poor cholesterol or homocysteine levels, with digestive issues, or focused on long-term prevention and anti-ageing. Not designed for elite athletes — those should look at {{PERFORMANCE_SENSOR}} instead.

## What is analyzed

### Topics covered by {{NUTRITION_SENSOR}}

Genetic factors relating to caffeine and coffee, Omega-3 and cholesterol, folic acid pathway, homocysteine protection, CoQ10 activation, detoxification of carcinogens, oxidative stress defence, selenium needs, salt sensitivity and blood pressure, Vitamin D3, lactose and calcium, inflammation, LDL and triglycerides, and iron absorption.

### Scientific basis

Every gene included in {{NUTRITION_SENSOR}} has been confirmed in at least three independent scientific studies. PubMed source IDs are listed at the back of each chapter in the report. Genetic traits are considered scientifically confirmed; derived recommendations are logical conclusions.

## Deliverables

### What the customer receives

A 200+ page personalised report explaining each genetic trait and its practical implications, a food rating list covering ~900–1,400 foods evaluated against the customer's profile, and a personalised micronutrient recipe that feeds into {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}} if ordered.

### Sample requirement

A single saliva sample collected at home using {{COMPANY_NAME}}'s proprietary saliva device. Avoid eating for 30 minutes before collection.
