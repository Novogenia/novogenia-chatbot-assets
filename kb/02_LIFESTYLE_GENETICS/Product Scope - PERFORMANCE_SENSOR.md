## Overview

### What is {{PERFORMANCE_SENSOR}}?

{{PERFORMANCE_SENSOR}} is a genetic analysis designed for intense athletes. Calorie distribution, antioxidant supply, and food ratings are adapted to higher physical demands. The analysis covers genetic factors related to stress response, muscle development, and physical endurance.

### Who is {{PERFORMANCE_SENSOR}} for?

Competitive athletes, performance-focused trainers, and people pursuing intense training schedules. Designed for athletic optimisation rather than general healthy eating — for that purpose, {{NUTRITION_SENSOR}} is the appropriate analysis.

## What is analyzed

### Topics covered by {{PERFORMANCE_SENSOR}}

Stress response and recovery genes, muscle development variants, endurance-related genes, athletic talent indicators, and higher-load nutrient needs (notably antioxidants, B-vitamins, and electrolytes).

## Deliverables

### What the customer receives

A ~500-page personalised report including the genetic Performance analysis, training-plan guidance, food ratings adapted for elevated training load, and inputs into {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}} dosing for athletic profiles.

### Relationship to {{NUTRITION_SENSOR}}

{{PERFORMANCE_SENSOR}} replaces {{NUTRITION_SENSOR}} for athletes — doing both is not meaningful. {{PERFORMANCE_SENSOR}} adapts ratings and dosing for higher-output bodies.

### Sample requirement

A single saliva sample collected at home using {{COMPANY_NAME}}'s proprietary saliva device.
