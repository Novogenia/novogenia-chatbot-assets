### Is the program's nutrition plan designed specifically for muscle gain?

The reply is: yes, of course! The recommended daily protein intake is 0.8g/kg body weight for a person without significant physical activity. If one wants to build muscle mass, the recommended daily amount is about 1.4g/kg body weight and can be even higher in certain sports. Here's an example: A person weighing 80kg would need to eat 0.8x80 = 64g of protein per day. If the same person now wants to gain muscle mass, they should increase their protein intake. The lowest amount of protein we recommend in the Athletic Performance DNA test is as follows: 14% for an 80kg athlete: 2500kcal per day, 14% of which should come from proteins. This corresponds to about 350kcal per day from proteins or approximately 85g of protein per day (1g of protein = approx. 4.1kcal). Moreover, if one trains regularly, they should also consume a protein resource right after training. So here we have 85g of protein from the diet PLUS 30g of protein directly after training, which totals 115g of protein per day. More protein may be needed for other sports. So yes, this program ensures that one consumes enough protein and is therefore optimal for building muscle mass.
