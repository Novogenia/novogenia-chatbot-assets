## Overview

### What the customer receives

The {{PERFORMANCE_SENSOR}} report is a roughly 80-page personalised document covering the genetic side of athletic performance: muscle fibre composition, oxygen-handling, recovery capacity, injury risk, and the personalised nutrient and training cues that follow. Designed for both recreational athletes and serious training.

### Tone and framing

Recommendations are written in the second person and presented as training-optimisation guidance rather than predictions of athletic outcome. The report emphasises that environment and training trump any single variant.

## Report sections

### Front matter

Foreword, brief intro to performance genetics, the standard disclaimer.

### Results overview

A "Your results at a glance" summary.

### Topic chapters

Muscle fibre type (ACTN3, ACE), VO₂max trainability, lactate clearance, injury risk (collagen / tendon variants), inflammation and recovery (IL6, TNFA), oxidative-stress handling under load (SOD2, GPX1), iron handling under endurance load (HFE), caffeine response (CYP1A2 — performance-relevant), and a personalised training plan summary.

### Closing pages

About-the-laboratory + technical details + training-plan appendix.

## Chapter structure

### How every chapter is built

Scientific intro, "most important details at a glance" box, gene-function explanation, customer's genotype + verdict, training and nutrition recommendation, PubMed citations.

### Verdict categories

Three frames per axis: "endurance-favoured" / "balanced" / "power-favoured" for fibre type, plus standard "favourable / moderate / high-impact" for recovery and injury-risk axes.

## Example findings

### Example 1 — sprinter profile

Topic: Muscle fibre type. Gene: ACTN3 (RR). Verdict: "Power-favoured." Narrative: ACTN3 RR is over-represented in elite sprinters and power athletes. Recommendation: structure training around strength and explosive work; endurance gains require deliberate aerobic blocks.

### Example 2 — endurance profile

Topic: ACE genotype. Verdict: "ACE II — endurance-favoured." Narrative: ACE II is associated with greater response to endurance training and better cardiac efficiency. Recommendation: lean into endurance work; strength is still important but is the secondary axis.

### Example 3 — recovery vulnerability

Topic: Inflammation under load. Gene: IL6 (−174C). Verdict: "Pro-inflammatory recovery profile." Narrative: stronger inflammatory response after hard sessions; recovery may take longer. Recommendation: protect sleep, omega-3, deliberate easy days, avoid excessive back-to-back high-intensity sessions.

### Example 4 — injury risk, dietary lever

Topic: Collagen / tendon. Gene: COL5A1 risk variant. Verdict: "Soft-tissue injury risk elevated." Narrative: associated with higher rates of Achilles and other tendon injuries. Recommendation: emphasise warm-up, eccentric strength work, gradual load progression; vitamin C and proline-rich nutrition support collagen synthesis.

### Example 5 — performance-relevant caffeine

Topic: Caffeine response. Gene: CYP1A2 (slow metaboliser). Verdict: "Caffeine clearance slow." Narrative: caffeine acts as a performance ergogenic but slow metabolisers can experience cardiovascular and sleep penalties at standard doses. Recommendation: limit pre-workout caffeine; avoid late-day intake even on race days.
