## Overview

### What the customer receives

The {{BIOLOGICAL_AGE_SENSOR}} report is a roughly 50-page personalised document covering the genetic side of biological ageing: telomere biology, methylation-cycle balance, oxidative-stress handling at the long-term level, and inflammation tone. The framing is preventive rather than predictive.

### Tone and framing

Recommendations are written in the second person and emphasise that ageing is the cumulative outcome of many small drivers. The report distinguishes genetic predisposition (modifiable in its consequences) from chronological age (unchangeable).

## Report sections

### Front matter

Foreword, brief intro to biological-age biology, the difference between biological and chronological age, and the standard disclaimer.

### Results overview

A "Your results at a glance" summary.

### Topic chapters

Telomere maintenance (TERT, BICD1), methylation-cycle integrity (MTHFR, MTR, MTRR, COMT), oxidative-stress defence (SOD2, GPX1), inflammation tone (IL6, TNFA, CRP, IL1A, IL1B), mitochondrial-function genes, and a personalised "longevity strategy" panel.

### Closing pages

About-the-laboratory + technical details.

## Chapter structure

### How every chapter is built

Scientific intro, "most important details at a glance" box, gene-function explanation, customer's genotype + verdict, recommendation paragraph, PubMed citations.

### Verdict categories

Three frames: "favourable" (no special intervention), "moderate variant" (moderate intervention recommended), "high-impact variant" (clear intervention recommended).

## Example findings

### Example 1 — favourable

Topic: Telomere maintenance. Gene: TERT (rs2735940). Verdict: "Favourable telomere-length variant." Narrative: associated with longer baseline telomeres; this is a strength to preserve through lifestyle.

### Example 2 — moderate variant, dietary nudge

Topic: Methylation. Gene: MTHFR (heterozygote). Verdict: "Folate activation moderately impaired." Narrative: reduced methylation capacity over decades contributes to elevated homocysteine and accelerated biological ageing. Recommendation: methylfolate in {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}} plus dietary leafy greens.

### Example 3 — high-impact, antioxidant network

Topic: Oxidative-stress network. Genes: SOD2 + GPX1 risk alleles. Verdict: "Reduced antioxidant reserve." Narrative: combined risk alleles reduce buffering against ROS; effects accumulate over decades. Recommendation: network antioxidant stack (vitamins C+E, polyphenols, selenium, manganese), regular movement, sleep prioritisation.

### Example 4 — elevated inflammation tone

Topic: Inflammation. Gene: IL6 (−174C). Verdict: "Pro-inflammatory IL-6 variant." Narrative: associated with higher baseline IL-6 production and elevated chronic-inflammation tendency. Recommendation: Mediterranean diet, omega-3, regular movement, limit ultra-processed foods.

### Example 5 — high-impact, modifiable

Topic: PPAR-gamma. Gene: PPARG (rs1801282 Pro12Ala). Verdict: "Insulin-sensitivity variant — Mediterranean-diet responsive." Narrative: the customer's PPARG variant interacts strongly with Mediterranean dietary pattern (PREDIMED-NAVARRA study) — Mediterranean adherence is associated with slower telomere shortening in carriers.
