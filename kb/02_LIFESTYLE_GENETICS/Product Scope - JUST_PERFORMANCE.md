## Overview

### What is {{JUST_PERFORMANCE}}?

{{JUST_PERFORMANCE}} is the standalone Performance analysis. Unlike {{PERFORMANCE_SENSOR}} which is sold as part of the Lifestyle bundle, {{JUST_PERFORMANCE}} is the Performance-only version for customers who already have a separate dietary plan and only want the performance-genetics layer.

### Who is {{JUST_PERFORMANCE}} for?

Athletes and trainers who already follow an established nutrition program and want only the performance-genetics insight, or customers who completed a different genetic analysis previously and want to add Performance without rebundling.

## What is analyzed

### Topics covered

Stress response and recovery genes, muscle-development variants, endurance-related genes, athletic talent indicators, and higher-load nutrient needs — same gene set as {{PERFORMANCE_SENSOR}}, packaged without the Lifestyle bundle context.

## Deliverables

### What the customer receives

A focused Performance report with training-plan guidance and athletic-load nutrient inputs. When combined with {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}}, performance results feed into the supplement formulation.

### Sample requirement

A single saliva sample collected at home using {{COMPANY_NAME}}'s proprietary saliva device.
