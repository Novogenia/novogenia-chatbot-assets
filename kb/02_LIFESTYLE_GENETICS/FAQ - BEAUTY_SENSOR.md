## Additional FAQs


### Collagen breakdown: The report recommends taking phytosterols. Should these be consumed through food, or does this refer to supplementation at higher doses as a nutritional supplement?

Phytosterols are mainly found in fatty plant parts. They are particularly rich in sunflower seeds, wheat germ, sesame, and soybeans as well as pumpkin seeds. They can be absorbed through food but can also be applied topically as a care product in creams. A study from 2008 was able to demonstrate the effect of topical application.

### How significant is the effect of hyaluronic acid on skin hydration? I have always had very dry skin, but according to my genetic analysis, my skin should retain moisture well.?

Studies show that skincare with hyaluronic acid is effective against wrinkles. In the skin, it acts like a water reservoir (1g of hyaluronic acid can bind 6 liters of water) and provides elasticity and firmness. It might be that the wrong facial care has always been used in this case, resulting in poor moisture retention. However, hyaluronic acid is not the same as hyaluronic acid, as the correct molecular size is crucial. Broadly speaking, we can distinguish between two sizes: high molecular weight hyaluronic acid and low molecular weight hyaluronic acid. The basic rule is: The smaller the hyaluronic chains, the deeper hyaluronic acid can penetrate the skin. Low molecular weight hyaluronic acid is thus more effective than high molecular weight hyaluronic acid. In cosmetic ingredients, both variants are called: Sodium Hyaluronate. Therefore, it is worthwhile to obtain further information from the manufacturer.

### How significant is the role of hyaluronic acid in maintaining skin moisture levels?

Hyaluronic acid is an important component of the extracellular matrix in connective tissue and plays a significant role in many functions due to its ability to store a large amount of water (6 liters per 1 gram of hyaluronic acid). It provides moisture, stability, and elasticity to connective tissue, keeping the skin firm and youthful.

In this case, it depends on the UV index of the day (the higher, the more SPF is needed), but unfortunately, there is no 100% protection as not all UV rays can be blocked. And it is also important here: This cream is only intended to protect against the usual daily amount and is not a substitute for longer stays in the sun. i. ### Oxidative stress: If Q10 activation is impaired, is it advisable to take ubiquinol instead of ubiquinone?

Ubiquinol is absorbed 3 to 8 times better than conventional ubiquinone (Q10) and is therefore more bioavailable. For this reason, a lower dose is sufficient to achieve the same effect. Many clinical studies have already demonstrated the superior effectiveness of ubiquinol compared to conventional Q10. The reason: To generate cell energy, the body must convert ubiquinol (reduced form) to ubiquinone. Without this conversion, the energy production process in the body cannot be completed, and energy reserves cannot be maintained. Therefore, both forms are essential. Since the NQO1 gene is impaired, it is important to take in other antioxidants to achieve full protection against free radicals.

### In another genetic test, a TERT gene was detected in a heterozygous form. Are there different types of TERT genes?

There is only one TERT gene but different variants, also called SNPs. SNPs are Single Nucleotide Polymorphisms and are located at different positions in the gene sequence. They can influence the resulting protein in different ways. In our assay, we examine the SNPs rs2242652 and rs2735940 of the TERT gene. Since you have both normal alleles (T/T) for rs2735940, your TERT function is normal.

### Carriers of a specific allele in the BICD1 gene have shorter telomeres, comparable to those of people 15-20 years older. Does this refer to the G/G variant?

The statement is partially correct. A study from 2008 was able to demonstrate that carriers with the homozygous risk allele C/C exhibited shorter telomeres than carriers of the normal allele. This shortening of 604 bp (± 204) corresponds to approximately 15-20 years. The G/G genotype is thus the "normal" genotype without additional risk.

### Does this only apply if there is also a polymorphism in the rs2242652 gene?
