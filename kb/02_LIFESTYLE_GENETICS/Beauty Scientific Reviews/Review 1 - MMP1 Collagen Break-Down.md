## Background

### The biology in one line

Dermal firmness depends on balanced collagen turnover. MMP-1 is the key enzyme that cleaves fibrillar collagen in skin, so genetic variation in MMP1 regulation is a plausible determinant of cutaneous aging susceptibility.

### Why MMP1 matters in aging skin

Photoaged skin shows a persistent shift toward matrix loss: MMPs go up, TGF-β/Smad-dependent procollagen synthesis goes down (Fisher 1999; Quan 2002, 2004). MMP-1 is the first cleaver of native fibrillar collagens — too much MMP-1 activity fragments collagen, lowers dermal mechanical strength, and shows up as visible aging.

### The vicious cycle

Fragmented collagen impairs fibroblast function, increases oxidative stress, further elevates MMP-1 — a self-reinforcing loop (Fisher 2009). Alpha-tocopherol attenuates age-related collagenase rises in fibroblasts (Ricciarelli 1999), implicating oxidative + PKC-dependent signaling.

## Functional significance of rs1799750 (1G/2G)

### What the variant does

rs1799750 is a guanine insertion/deletion at MMP1 promoter position 1607. The 2G allele creates an Ets-binding site adjacent to an AP-1 responsive region — this raises promoter activity and transcriptional responsiveness compared to 1G (Fujimoto 2002; Tower 2002, 2003).

### How to interpret the genotype

Not a deterministic lesion — a regulatory susceptibility variant. The 2G effect is largest under UV / oxidative stress / AP-1-driven inflammatory signaling. 1G/1G individuals have the most blunted inducible response.

## Evidence from human studies

### Wrinkle and pelvic-tissue links

- Vierkötter 2015 (SALIA cohort, 697 elderly women): wrinkle severity + airflow obstruction clustered in 2G carriers — shared extracellular-matrix aging susceptibility.
- Ferrari 2012: MMP1 promoter polymorphism associated with pelvic organ prolapse susceptibility (broader connective-tissue relevance).
- Diniz-Fernandes 2018: MMP1 variants linked to collagen III / V remodeling in tendinopathy — same logic in non-skin tissues.

### What this means for skin

A higher-expression MMP1 genotype matters most when collagen is already under stress (UV, oxidative injury, low-grade inflammation). The 2G allele tips the balance further toward net matrix loss → progressive wrinkle formation.

---

## Mechanism-based interventions

### Strongest mechanistic support

Lutein, vitamin C, vitamin E, and selected phytosterols (Fujimoto 2002; Tower 2002, 2003; Philips 2007; Nusgens 2001; Ricciarelli 1999; Hwang 2014; Beitner 2003). The strategy: reduce inducible MMP-1 activity while preserving the procollagen-synthesis arm of collagen homeostasis.

### Why sun protection comes first

Because the 2G effect is UV-induced, sunscreen is the upstream lever. Antioxidants are adjunctive.
