## The 9 Beauty Reviews

### Review 1 — MMP1 / Collagen Break-Down

Gene: MMP1 (rs1799750 / 1G/2G). Topic: collagenase activity → wrinkle formation. 2G allele = higher inducible MMP-1 expression under UV stress. Interventions: lutein, vitamin C, vitamin E, phytosterols.

### Review 2 — CYP1A2 / Collagen Production

Gene: CYP1A2 (rs762551 / -163C>A). Topic: caffeine metabolism speed → prolidase suppression → collagen biosynthesis. Slow metabolizers more vulnerable. Interventions: hydrolyzed collagen, vitamin C, folic acid + caffeine moderation.

### Review 3 — MC1R / UV Protection

Gene: MC1R (rs1805006, rs1805007, rs11547464, rs885479). Topic: melanocyte UV defense — pigmentation, antioxidant response, DNA repair. Interventions: sunscreen (primary), combined topical vitamins C + E.

### Review 4 — MC1R / Skin Hydration

Same gene as Review 3. Topic: UVB → disruption of cutaneous hyaluronic acid metabolism → dryness. Interventions: sun protection + HA-containing skincare.

### Review 5 — GSTs, SOD2, GPX1 / Oxidative Stress

Genes: GSTM1, GSTT1, GSTP1 (rs1695), SOD2 (rs4880), GPX1 (rs1050450). Topic: integrated antioxidant defense network. Interventions: vitamins C + E (combined topical), alpha-lipoic acid, zinc, manganese.

### Review 6 — NQO1 / Coenzyme Q10

Gene: NQO1 (rs1800566 / Pro187Ser). Topic: quinone reduction → Q10 activation (ubiquinone → ubiquinol). Reduced-function carriers benefit less from Q10 alone. Interventions: Q10 + vitamins C/E + alpha-lipoic acid + zinc + manganese as a network.

### Review 7 — GPX1 / Selenium Requirement

Gene: GPX1 (rs1050450). Topic: selenium-dependent peroxide detoxification. Selenium availability determines genotype expression. Interventions: selenium optimization + glutathione support (NAC) + topical antioxidants. (Source = V1 draft state.)

### Review 8 — Inflammation (TNFA, IL1A/B, IL6, IL6R, CRP)

Genes: TNFA, IL1A, IL1B, IL6, IL6R, CRP. Topic: post-UV inflammatory amplification → photoaging. Interventions: omega-3 + arachidonic-acid moderation (primary); MSM, resveratrol, vitamin D, vitamin C, zinc (adjunctive).

### Review 9 — TERT, BICD1, PPARG / Telomeres & Biological Age

Genes: TERT (rs2735940, rs2242652), BICD1 (rs2630578), PPARG (rs1801282 / Pro12Ala). Topic: telomere maintenance + biological aging. PPARG Pro12Ala is diet-responsive (Mediterranean pattern protective). Interventions: Mediterranean diet, antioxidants, omega-3, sun protection.
