## Background

### The biology in one line

Collagen renewal depends on both new synthesis and recycling of proline / hydroxyproline via prolidase. Caffeine suppresses both, and CYP1A2 genotype determines how long the customer is exposed to caffeine.

### The mechanism

Caffeine reduces collagen biosynthesis in dermal fibroblasts and lowers prolidase activity dose-dependently (Palka & Phang 1997; Surazynski 2008; Donejko 2014). Reduced caffeine clearance → longer exposure → sustained interference with collagen homeostasis.

## Functional significance of CYP1A2 rs762551

### What the variant does

rs762551 (-163C>A) is a regulatory polymorphism modifying CYP1A2 activity and inducibility (NOT abolishing it). Genotype-dependent differences in caffeine clearance show most clearly under regular / high caffeine intake (Koonrungsesomboon 2018; Djordjevic 2010; Cornelis 2006; Palatini 2009).

### How to read it

- A/A "fast metabolizer" — efficient caffeine clearance, less sustained interference with collagen pathways.
- C-carrier ("slow metabolizer") — prolonged caffeine exposure, more vulnerable to caffeine-driven collagen suppression.

## Human evidence

### Cohort and pharmacogenetic findings

- Koonrungsesomboon 2018 meta-analysis (n=3,570): rs762551 most consistent functional determinant; A-allele carriers showed greater caffeine metabolism, especially among smokers.
- Djordjevic 2010 (Serbian + Swedish nonsmokers): heavy coffee → higher CYP1A2 activity, predominantly in -163A/A carriers — genotype-dependent inducibility.
- Cornelis 2006 (Costa Rica, n=2,014 MI cases + controls): coffee-related cardiac risk confined to slow metabolizers — biological importance of genotype confirmed.
- Palatini 2009 (553 White adults): coffee-hypertension link varied by CYP1A2 genotype.
- Guessous 2012 (cohorts n=16,719): rs762551 C allele = lower CYP1A2 activity in non-smokers; genotype effect on BP modified by caffeine intake.
- Yoshihara 2019 (RCT n=201): in low habitual caffeine consumers, CC carriers had greater systolic BP response to caffeine than AA/CA — confirms genotype-specific sensitivity.

## Supportive interventions

### Hydrolyzed collagen (oral)

Bioactive peptides reach blood and skin, associated with improved wrinkles, hydration, dermal matrix synthesis (Proksch 2014; Asserin 2015; de Miranda 2021; Yazaki 2017; Lee 2022).

### Vitamin C

Stimulates collagen synthesis, enhances type I and III collagen gene expression in fibroblasts, topical application raises dermal collagens / processing enzymes / TIMP-1 (Murad 1981; Tajima & Pinnell 1996; Nusgens 2001).

### Folic acid

Penetrates skin, improves firmness, supports nucleotide excision repair (Knott 2007; Fischer 2011; Burger 2007). Dermal integrity needs both collagen synthesis and efficient cellular repair.

## Talking points

### What to tell a customer who is a slow metabolizer

"Your CYP1A2 gene metabolizes caffeine slowly — caffeine stays in your system longer and that has a subtle suppressive effect on collagen recycling. We don't need to ban coffee, but moderation plus collagen-supportive nutrients (vitamin C, folate, hydrolyzed collagen) is your best lever."

### What to tell a fast metabolizer

"Your CYP1A2 clears caffeine quickly — this isn't where your main collagen lever sits. The general anti-aging stack (vitamin C, sun protection) still applies."
