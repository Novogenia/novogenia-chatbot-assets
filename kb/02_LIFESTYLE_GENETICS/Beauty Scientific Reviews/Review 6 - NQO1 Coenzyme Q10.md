## Background

### What NQO1 does

NQO1 is a cytosolic FAD-dependent oxidoreductase that uses NADH / NADPH to reduce quinones to hydroquinones in a single two-electron step. This is biologically important because one-electron quinone reduction produces semiquinones that re-enter redox cycling and amplify oxidative stress. NQO1 prevents that — it is a detoxification + redox-buffering enzyme (Ross 2000; Traver 1997).

### Why it matters for Q10

Q10 has two forms: ubiquinone (oxidized) and ubiquinol (reduced, antioxidant-active). Ubiquinol is the chain-breaking antioxidant in membranes. Reduced NQO1 → less efficient activation/recycling of Q10 toward ubiquinol pool (Fischer 2011).

### Why it matters for skin

Skin is continuously oxidatively challenged (UV, pollution, inflammation). The epidermal Nrf2 axis induces NQO1 as part of the photoprotective response. Studies in keratinocytes show NQO1 participates in the UV-stress response (Marrot 2008; Kim 2014; Takei 2015).

---

## Functional significance of rs1800566 (Pro187Ser)

### What the variant does

Missense polymorphism causing Pro→Ser substitution. Impairs protein stability, disrupts native conformation, promotes rapid ubiquitination + proteasomal degradation → reduced cellular pool of catalytically competent NQO1 (Siegel 2001; Lienhart 2014).

### Genotype-activity relationship

Heterozygotes show intermediate enzyme activity; homozygous reduced-function genotypes near-complete deficiency (Traver 1997; Siegel 1999). Confirmed associations with disease susceptibility in oxidatively-stressed tissues — functionally meaningful, not just statistical marker (Freriksen 2014).

## Consequences for cutaneous biology

### What happens with reduced NQO1

Weakened redox balance maintenance → less efficient ubiquinol activation → more ROS amplification under UV + pollution exposure. Downstream: enhanced inflammatory signaling, increased MMP expression, collagen fragmentation, declining tissue resilience — self-reinforcing oxidative cycle (Rinnerthaler 2015; Fisher 2009; Papaccio 2022).

---

## Mechanism-based interventions

### Coenzyme Q10 — directly pathway-aligned

Topical Q10 reaches viable epidermal layers, raises cutaneous Q10 levels, shows antioxidant effects. Human data: improved wrinkle appearance + skin smoothness (Inui 2008; Knott 2015; Žmitek 2017).

### The catch — Q10 alone is not enough

Because endogenous quinone-reducing capacity is attenuated in reduced-function NQO1 genotypes, Q10 alone leaves part of its antioxidant potential locked up in the oxidized form. Broader antioxidant support is needed.

### Combined network strategy

Q10 (core, pathway-aligned) + adjunctive compounds acting via independent pathways:
- Vitamin C + E (chain-breaking antioxidants, regenerate Q10).
- Alpha-lipoic acid (regenerates other antioxidants).
- Zinc, manganese (cofactors for endogenous enzymes).

This network approach is biologically more appropriate than exclusive reliance on Q10 alone for reduced-function NQO1 genotypes.
