## Summary for the bot

### What this review is about

This review covers a network of five oxidative-defense genes — GSTM1, GSTT1, GSTP1, SOD2, GPX1 — that collectively shape how well the skin handles reactive oxygen species. Variation across this network determines how cumulative oxidative injury translates into visible aging.

### Headline takeaway

Reduced-function genotypes need stronger antioxidant support. Most pathway-aligned: vitamins C + E, alpha-lipoic acid, zinc, manganese.

## Background

### The oxidative aging axis

Intrinsic aging + UV / pollution / inflammation generate ROS in skin. When production exceeds antioxidant buffering, damage accumulates in lipids, proteins, DNA, and ECM — driving wrinkles, elasticity loss, dyschromia, impaired repair (Rinnerthaler 2015; Kammeyer & Luiten 2015; Poljšak 2012).

### The five genes act in sequence

- Mitochondrial superoxide → **SOD2** (MnSOD) → H₂O₂.
- H₂O₂ → **GPX1** (selenium-dependent) → water.
- Reactive electrophiles (e.g. oxidized lipids) → **GSTM1**, **GSTT1**, **GSTP1** detoxify via glutathione conjugation.

## Glutathione S-transferase variants (GSTM1, GSTT1)

### What the variants are

GSTM1 and GSTT1 polymorphisms are typically homozygous gene DELETIONS — null genotype = no enzyme expression, lower phase II detoxification reserve.

### Functional consequence

In cutaneous tissue, oxidative stress generates electrophilic secondary products (lipid-peroxidation derivatives). Without GSTM1/T1, these accumulate and damage structural proteins, membranes, redox-sensitive signaling. Chronic UV / pollution / smoking amplify the impact (McWilliams 1995; Wenzlaff 2005; Ghelli 2021).

---

## GSTP1 rs1695

### What the variant does

In contrast to the deletion polymorphisms above, rs1695 is a missense variant in GSTP1 — alters amino acid sequence, modifies catalytic behavior and substrate handling (Watson 1998; Sreeja 2008).

### Why it matters for skin

Reduced GSTP1 efficiency → impaired neutralization of electrophilic oxidative by-products → cumulative molecular damage over time → modifier of oxidative-stress-driven skin aging (Watson 1998; do Nascimento 2021).

---

## SOD2 rs4880

### What the variant does

SOD2 encodes mitochondrial Mn-SOD — converts superoxide to H₂O₂. rs4880 sits in the mitochondrial targeting sequence — affects mitochondrial import efficiency AND mRNA stability → modulates effective antioxidant performance (Sutton 2003, 2005; Paludo 2014).

### Why it matters

Mitochondrial oxidative stress drives fibroblast dysfunction, senescence signaling, ECM deterioration (Treiber 2012). Less favorable rs4880 genotype lowers the threshold at which mitochondrial oxidative burden accumulates.

---

## GPX1 (selenium-dependent)

### What it does

GPX1 = glutathione peroxidase 1, detoxifies H₂O₂ and lipid hydroperoxides using selenium as a cofactor. Variation in GPX1 is covered in depth in Review 7 — Selenium Requirement.

## Mechanism-based interventions

### Supportive antioxidants

- **Vitamin C + E**: pathway-congruent antioxidants, especially combined topical C + E shows synergistic photoprotective effect.
- **Alpha-lipoic acid**: regenerates other antioxidants, supports mitochondrial redox balance.
- **Zinc + manganese**: cofactors for endogenous antioxidant systems (Mn for SOD2, Zn for Cu/Zn-SOD).

### Strategic framing

These compounds compensate for reduced endogenous antioxidant capacity. Reduced GSTM1/T1/P1, SOD2, or GPX1 function = stronger rationale for both topical and systemic antioxidant support (Al-Niaimi & Chiang 2017; Beitner 2003; Rostan 2002).
