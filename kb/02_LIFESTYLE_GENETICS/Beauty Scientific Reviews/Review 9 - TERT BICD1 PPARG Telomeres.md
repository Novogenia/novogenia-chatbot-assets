## Summary for the bot

### What this review is about

Three genes acting at distinct but converging levels on telomere-driven skin aging: TERT (direct telomerase regulation), BICD1 (indirect telomere homeostasis via trafficking), PPARG (metabolic + nutrigenomic modulation). The variants discussed: TERT rs2735940 and rs2242652; BICD1 rs2630578; PPARG rs1801282 (Pro12Ala).

### Headline takeaway

Telomere maintenance shapes skin's long-term renewal capacity. Variation in these genes modifies the pace of biological aging. Diet (especially Mediterranean dietary patterns) interacts with PPARG genotype to shape telomere dynamics — making nutrition a real lever, especially for Pro12Ala carriers.

## Background

### Why telomeres matter for skin

Skin is a high-turnover tissue. Telomere erosion limits replicative capacity. Epidermal stem-cell behavior is highly sensitive to telomere integrity. Once critically short, telomeres trigger senescence programs → reduced regenerative reserve + reduced ECM homeostasis → visible aging (Kosmadaki & Gilchrest 2004; Boukamp 2005; Buckingham & Klingelhutz 2011).

### Evidence specific to skin

- Telomerase dosage + telomere integrity affect epidermal stem-cell behavior (Flores 2005).
- Longer genetically predicted telomere length → less facial skin aging (Liu 2019; Zhan & Hägg 2021).
- UVA exposure accelerates telomere shortening in dermal fibroblasts (Yin & Jiang 2013).

## TERT — direct telomerase regulation

### What TERT does

TERT encodes the catalytic reverse-transcriptase subunit of telomerase. Telomerase counteracts progressive telomere shortening. Inherited differences in TERT regulation directly affect long-term replicative reserve of proliferative tissues including epidermis (Matsubara 2006a, 2006b; Boukamp 2005; Buckingham & Klingelhutz 2011).

### Variant rs2735940 (best characterized)

Promoter variation. Associated with altered transcriptional activity and differences in leukocyte telomere length — variant modifies transcriptional output, not just a linked marker (Matsubara 2006a; Ludlow 2008).

### Variant rs2242652

Telomere-relevant locus marker within a broader TERT susceptibility region. Repeatedly associated with telomere-related phenotypes and cancer risk, including skin cancer outcomes (Campa 2015; Nan 2011; Bojesen 2013). Direct functional consequences less clearly resolved than rs2735940.

### Cutaneous consequence

Diminished telomerase support → faster acquisition of critically short telomeres → replicative senescence in keratinocyte + fibroblast lineages → reduced regenerative reserve → impaired recovery from oxidative and UV injury → accelerated biological skin aging.

## BICD1 — indirect telomere modulation

### What BICD1 does

Member of the Bicaudal-D family of dynein motor adaptors. Functions in cargo coupling, microtubule-based transport, endosomal sorting. Not a telomerase component (Terenzio 2014; Hoogenraad & Akhmanova 2016).

### Variant rs2630578

Regulatory polymorphism associated with interindividual variation in telomere length AND reduced BICD1 mRNA expression. Mechanism is regulatory (altered expression) rather than protein-coding (Mangino 2008).

### How to interpret

A telomere-length modifier acting indirectly. Carriers may have earlier accumulation of senescent cells in proliferative + reparative compartments → reduced renewal, impaired tissue homeostasis, lower resilience to photodamage.

## PPARG — metabolic + nutrigenomic modulation

### What PPARG does

Ligand-activated nuclear receptor regulating adipogenesis, insulin sensitivity, lipid handling, inflammatory tone, cellular differentiation. In skin: contributes to keratinocyte differentiation, barrier function, sebocyte biology, inflammatory homeostasis (Ramot 2015; Mao-Qiang 2004).

### Variant rs1801282 (Pro12Ala)

Missense substitution altering receptor activity and downstream metabolic signaling. Established associations with insulin sensitivity and diet response (Deeb 1998; Ramot 2015).

### Why this matters for skin aging

Effect on biological aging is INDIRECT — through metabolic control, inflammation, oxidative balance, nutrient responsiveness rather than direct telomerase activation.

### The key evidence — gene × diet interaction

García-Calzón 2015: dietary intervention data show PPARG Pro12Ala modifies the relationship between Mediterranean dietary exposure and telomere shortening. Tran 2021 reinforces PPARG2 Pro12Ala as a diet-sensitive locus in chronic disease biology.

### Practical interpretation

For Pro12Ala carriers, cutaneous aging pathways may respond MORE strongly to dietary quality — especially through lipid metabolism, barrier integrity, low-grade inflammation. Diet becomes a real anti-aging lever for this genotype.

## Pathway-supportive interventions

### General principle

These three loci are NOT corrected by intervention. They modify the efficiency of telomere-related biological pathways, which remain biologically responsive to nutritional + metabolic modulation. Strategy = pathway support + risk-factor reduction (Buckingham & Klingelhutz 2011; Galiè 2020; Tran 2021).

### What helps

- **Mediterranean dietary pattern** — strongest evidence for telomere preservation, especially with PPARG Pro12Ala.
- **Antioxidants** (vitamins, phytochemicals) — limit ROS-driven telomere shortening.
- **Omega-3 fatty acids** — modulate inflammatory tone, indirect telomere preservation.
- **Sun protection** — UVA accelerates fibroblast telomere shortening; sunscreen + topical antioxidants help.

### What to say to a PPARG Pro12Ala carrier

"Your PPARG variant makes your skin aging especially responsive to diet — Mediterranean-style eating (olive oil, fatty fish, vegetables, less processed food) shows real telomere protection effects in carriers like you. This is one of the cleanest gene-diet stories in skin biology."
