## Overview

### What is {{BEAUTY_SENSOR}}?

{{BEAUTY_SENSOR}} is a genetic analysis of 20+ skin-related genes covering collagen production and breakdown, UV protection, skin moisture, oxidative stress, CoQ10 effectiveness, selenium needs, inflammation risk, and biological skin age. Results inform both supplementation and personalised cosmetic formulations.

### Who is {{BEAUTY_SENSOR}} for?

People interested in skin health and anti-ageing, customers wanting personalised skincare beyond off-the-shelf products, and those who want their cosmetics formulated against their actual skin genetics rather than generic skin types.

## What is analyzed

### Topics covered by {{BEAUTY_SENSOR}}

Collagen synthesis and breakdown, UV protection capacity, hyaluronic acid and moisture retention, antioxidant defence (including CoQ10 effectiveness), selenium requirement, inflammation tendency, and genetic biological skin age.

## Deliverables

### What the customer receives

A personalised report explaining each skin gene and its practical implication, dietary and supplementation recommendations (e.g. phytosterol for collagen support), and — when paired with {{COSMETICS_DAY_NIGHT_95ML}} or {{COSMETICS_BODY_LOTION_235ML}} — a fully personalised cosmetic formulation drawn from over 30 billion possible recipes.

### Sample requirement

A single saliva sample collected at home using {{COMPANY_NAME}}'s proprietary saliva device.
