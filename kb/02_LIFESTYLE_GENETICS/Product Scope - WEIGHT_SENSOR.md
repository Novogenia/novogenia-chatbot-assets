## Overview

### What is {{WEIGHT_SENSOR}}?

{{WEIGHT_SENSOR}} is a genetic analysis identifying an individual's strengths and weaknesses related to body weight. It determines whether carbohydrates or fats drive weight gain for this person, and whether exercise or calorie reduction is more effective — informing the customer's personalised weight-management strategy.

### Who is {{WEIGHT_SENSOR}} for?

People who struggle to lose weight, who plateau on standard diets, who want to understand why certain approaches do or don't work for them, or who want a science-based weight strategy instead of generic advice.

## What is analyzed

### Topics covered by {{WEIGHT_SENSOR}}

Fat sensitivity, carbohydrate sensitivity, effectiveness of exercise vs. calorie reduction, yo-yo tendency, muscle loss tendency, visceral fat accumulation risk, basal metabolic rate, and sport/calorie type classification. Eight core genes underlie the analysis; combined with body and lifestyle factors there are over 6,500 possible outcome profiles.

### Internal study result

An internal study with 300 participants showed 2.5× more weight loss with the genetic program compared with standard strategies, at the same effort level.

## Deliverables

### What the customer receives

A 100+ page personalised report, the customer's weight-loss strategy chosen from over 3,500 possible outcomes, 900+ foods rated for that profile, 40 daily menus, 100 recipes (the {{RECIPE_BOOK}}), and a personalised exercise plan.

### Validity and reorder

The genetic profile does not change, so the analysis is valid for life. The interpretation is continuously improved as new science is integrated — typically weekly updates to the algorithm.

### Sample requirement

A single saliva sample collected at home using {{COMPANY_NAME}}'s proprietary saliva device.
