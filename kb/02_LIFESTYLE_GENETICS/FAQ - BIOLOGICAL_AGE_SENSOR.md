## {{BIOLOGICAL_AGE_SENSOR}}

### How does the {{BIOLOGICAL_AGE_SENSOR}} work?

SNPs in genes associated with telomere length and elongation are analyzed (e.g. TERT, BICD1, PPARG). From these results, the body's genetic capacity to lengthen telomeres is estimated. This is not a direct measurement of telomere length.

## Additional FAQs


### How does your test determine the body's ability to lengthen telomeres and what new capabilities are anticipated with the upgrade to analyze up to 700,000 SNPs?

In our report, we examine genetic variants (SNPs) of specially selected genes. Based on the resulting outcomes, we indicate the body's ability to lengthen telomeres based on these genes. We do not conduct a telomere length determination in this case. We are currently establishing a new system that will allow us to analyze up to 700,000 SNPs per customer in one run. All our existing Tests will also be expanded accordingly in the near future. In the case of the Biological age DNA test, the following SNPs would be involved: TERT - rs2242652 - rs2735940 BICD1 - rs2630578 PPARG - rs1801282
