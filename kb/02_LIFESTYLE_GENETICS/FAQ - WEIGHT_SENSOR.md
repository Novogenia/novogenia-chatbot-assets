### Isn't a calorie deficit ultimately the deciding factor for weight loss?

Not on its own. Genetics shows that people respond differently to fat and carbohydrates — some barely gain weight on the same caloric surplus that causes significant weight gain in others. The effectiveness of exercise for weight loss also varies genetically by up to a factor of 3 between individuals.

### Are there 7 dietary types in the {{WEIGHT_SENSOR}}?

Yes: Fat-Burner (higher fat ratio), Mixed I–V (mixed types with various distributions), and Carb-Burner (higher carbohydrate ratio). Combined with individual factors such as basal metabolic rate, exercise effectiveness, and yo-yo tendency, there are over 6,500 possible genetic profiles in total.

### You state that consuming more calories than expended leads to weight gain, regardless of genetic food compatibility. How does this align with genetic weight loss principles?

This view is already over ten years old and is called the calorie balance. If you put 2000 kcal into the body and burn 1500 kcal, you gain 500 kcal per day. Mathematically, this makes absolute sense, but it cannot be proven in studies. Some people eat twice as many calories and burn half as many calories through exercise and do not gain weight, while for others it is exactly the opposite. The probably most beautiful example is research on fat sensitivity: The Robertaille research group had 720 participants follow a more calorie and more fat diet over several months in 2003. Some reacted as their experts say: They gradually gained weight. However, a certain percentage did not gain weight despite a calorie surplus and more fat in the diet. For these individuals, the calorie balance does not seem to match. Six different genes have already been associated with this effect. Another research group repeated the same experiment in 2003 with over 2000 participants and found the same result again: some gain weight with more calories from fat, while others do not. This also means that people who are genetically insensitive to fat do not have to expect weight gain if they increase fat content and reduce carbohydrates, which are sometimes the problem.
