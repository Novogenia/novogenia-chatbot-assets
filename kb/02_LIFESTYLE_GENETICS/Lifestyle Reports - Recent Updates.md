## General improvements across all lifestyle reports

### Clear table of contents

The old chapter overview sheet has been replaced with a proper table of contents with page numbers — customers and clinicians can jump straight to a chapter rather than scrolling.

### Food list reorganised into 10 categories

The 900+ rated foods are now split into ten clear categories: Fruit, Vegetables, Oils/Spices, Meat/Sausages, Fish, Dairy Products, Bread/Baked Goods/Cereals, Sweets, Beverages including Alcohol, and Menu Components. Replaces the old single long list.

### Two-part report structure

Every report is now divided into two main parts: (1) a summary of results at the beginning, then (2) detailed information per chapter. The detailed part of each chapter contains: background knowledge, an explanatory video from Dr. Daniel Wallerstorfer (via QR code, for almost all topics), an explanation of the in-body processes for the relevant gene, and the associated scientific basis.

### Version history reset

The internal report version number has been reset and now starts at **1.1**, increasing with every product-management update. The last number on the old report generation was **538**.

### Summary-of-results format

The new results summary uses: (1) a clear traffic-light overview red→green, (2) a meaningful result heading per chapter, (3) a short description of the result, (4) a reference to the detailed information chapter where the customer can read more.

## Product-specific innovations

### {{NUTRITION_SENSOR}} — content reorganised and three new analyses added

Nutrition genes are now grouped by category (Nutrigenetics, Metabolism, Brain, …) and consolidated under "Your total requirement", instead of being listed one by one. Three new analyses joined the report:

- **Immune System — properly adjusted?** Genes: TNFA (rs1800629), IL6 (rs1800795), IL1RN (rs419598), CRP (rs3093066), IL6R (rs2228145).
- **Iron Intake — normal or too much?** Genes: HFE (rs1799945), HFE (rs1800730), HFE (rs1800562).
- **Detoxification of burned/pollutants** — how harmful is nicotine, grilled, and heavily fried food for me. Genes: CYP1A1 (rs4646903), CYP1A1 (rs1048943), CYP1B1 (rs1056836).

### {{WEIGHT_SENSOR}} Standard — content removed

Two items that used to be in the Weight Sensor are no longer included: the "Genetic predisposition to obesity" chapter and the "Optimal pulse rate" recommendation.

### {{WEIGHT_SENSOR}} — daily menus moved

Daily menus are no longer in the standard Weight Sensor report. They are now only in the personalised recipe book ({{RECIPE_BOOK}}).

### {{WEIGHT_SENSOR}} — diet types renamed and macronutrient distribution standardised

The old fat-/carb-metaboliser labels have been renamed, and the macronutrient splits are now explicit. Protein is held at 20% across all types; fat and carbohydrates shift across the spectrum:

| Diet type | Fat | Carbs | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Very Good Fat Metaboliser | 35% | 45% | 20% |
| Good Fat Metaboliser | 33% | 47% | 20% |
| Fat Metaboliser Type | 31% | 49% | 20% |
| Mixed Type I | 29% | 51% | 20% |
| Mixed Type II | 27% | 53% | 20% |
| Mixed Type III | 25% | 55% | 20% |
| Carbohydrate Metaboliser | 20% | 60% | 20% |

### {{WEIGHT_SENSOR}} — new clear overall result pages

New clear pages now sit at the top of the Weight Sensor result block summarising the three core axes — Diet type, Exercise type, Weight-loss strategy — each with action recommendations at a glance.

### {{TOXO_SENSOR}} — consolidated detoxification chapters

The detoxification results have been consolidated. Old structure had multiple sub-results per Phase; new structure presents one consolidated result per detoxification topic:

- **Old:** Phase 1 detoxification of exhaust gases, nicotine smoke, fried/grilled food — split into 2 results (Effectiveness of Phase 1 + Detoxification of ash/soot/smoke).
- **New:** **One result** — Detoxification of burnt substances / Detoxification of Pollutants.

And for chemicals:

- **Old:** Phase 2 detoxification of pesticides and heavy metals — 3 results (Effectiveness of Phase 2, Detoxification of heavy metals, Detoxification of pesticides/chemicals/fungicides/herbicides/insect sprays).
- **New:** **One result** — Detoxification of Chemicals.

### {{TOXO_SENSOR}} combined with {{NUTRITION_SENSOR}}

When the {{TOXO_SENSOR}} is ordered together with the {{NUTRITION_SENSOR}}, the Toxo report now also includes overlapping chapters from the Nutrition Sensor: Coffee and Caffeine, Coenzyme Q10 (the anti-ageing molecule), Detoxification of Burnt Substances, Detoxification of Chemicals, Protection against Ageing from Oxidative Stress, Adequate Selenium Supply — and the Toxo-specific Drugs & Alcohol chapter.

### {{BIOLOGICAL_AGE_SENSOR}}, {{BURNOUT_SENSOR}}, Epigenetics Information

Content and design improvements have been applied (the general improvements on pages 2–3). No product-specific structural changes.

## How the bot should use this

### When a returning customer notices changes

"Yes — we recently rolled out a new version of the genetic lifestyle reports. The biggest visible changes are a proper table of contents, a cleaner two-part structure (summary first, then detailed chapters), and 10 food-list categories instead of one long list. The version number on your new report starts at 1.1 — the old generation's last number was 538."

### When a customer asks what new analyses they get with {{NUTRITION_SENSOR}}

Three are new in the latest version: Immune System (TNFA / IL6 / IL1RN / CRP / IL6R), Iron Intake (HFE variants — three of them), and Detoxification of Burned Substances (CYP1A1 + CYP1B1).

### When a {{WEIGHT_SENSOR}} customer asks about daily menus

The daily menus moved out of the standard Weight Sensor report into the personalised {{RECIPE_BOOK}}. The Weight Sensor itself now focuses on the genetic findings + diet type, exercise type, and weight-loss strategy summaries.

### When a {{WEIGHT_SENSOR}} customer asks about diet types

The new diet types are: Very Good Fat Metaboliser, Good Fat Metaboliser, Fat Metaboliser Type, Mixed Type I/II/III, Carbohydrate Metaboliser. Protein is always 20%; fat and carbs shift along the spectrum.

### When a {{TOXO_SENSOR}} customer asks why their report looks simpler

The detox chapters have been deliberately consolidated — one clear result per topic instead of multiple sub-results. The same gene analyses run underneath, but the customer-facing finding is one clear answer per detoxification area.

### What did NOT change

{{PERFORMANCE_SENSOR}} is not part of this update generation. The four named lifestyle sensors above plus {{BIOLOGICAL_AGE_SENSOR}}, {{BURNOUT_SENSOR}}, and Epigenetics all got the general improvements; the product-specific changes apply only to Nutrition, Weight, and Toxo.
