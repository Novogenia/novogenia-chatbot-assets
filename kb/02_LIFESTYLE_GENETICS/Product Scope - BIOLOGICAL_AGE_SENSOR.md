## Overview

### What is {{BIOLOGICAL_AGE_SENSOR}}?

{{BIOLOGICAL_AGE_SENSOR}} analyses SNPs in genes associated with telomere length and elongation — including TERT, BICD1, and PPARG. From these results, the body's genetic capacity to maintain or lengthen telomeres is estimated. It is not a direct telomere-length measurement.

### Who is {{BIOLOGICAL_AGE_SENSOR}} for?

People interested in longevity and anti-ageing, those building a long-term prevention plan, and customers who want to understand their underlying cellular-ageing tendency rather than just chronological age.

## What is analyzed

### Topics covered by {{BIOLOGICAL_AGE_SENSOR}}

Telomere maintenance genes (TERT, BICD1, PPARG, and related variants), genetic ageing tendency, and inputs that flow into anti-ageing dietary and supplement recommendations.

## Deliverables

### What the customer receives

A focused report explaining the customer's genetic ageing tendency and recommendations for diet, supplementation, and lifestyle to support telomere maintenance. Findings feed into {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}} dosing for antioxidants and other longevity-supportive nutrients.

### Sample requirement

A single saliva sample collected at home using {{COMPANY_NAME}}'s proprietary saliva device.
