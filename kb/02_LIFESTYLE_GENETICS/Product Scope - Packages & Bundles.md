## One-time DNA-test bundles

### {{PACKAGE_SHAPE}} — Shape (one-time)

A one-time bundle including {{WEIGHT_SENSOR}} plus the personalised {{RECIPE_BOOK}}. Customers receive their Weight Sensor genetic analysis and the 100-recipe / 40-menu personalised cookbook generated from their results. No supplement subscription is included.

### {{PACKAGE_LIFESTYLE}} — Lifestyle (one-time)

A one-time bundle including {{WEIGHT_SENSOR}}, {{NUTRITION_SENSOR}}, {{TOXO_SENSOR}}, {{BURNOUT_SENSOR}}, {{BIOLOGICAL_AGE_SENSOR}}, and the {{RECIPE_BOOK}}. The customer receives the full Lifestyle DNA analysis as a single print + digital report (typically 400+ pages). No supplement subscription is included.

### {{PACKAGE_PERFORMANCE}} — Performance (one-time)

The Lifestyle bundle plus {{PERFORMANCE_SENSOR}}, designed for intense athletes. Same DNA-only structure as Lifestyle, with the Performance layer adding training-plan guidance and athletic-load nutrient recommendations.

### {{PACKAGE_BEAUTY}} — Beauty (one-time)

A one-time bundle containing the {{BEAUTY_SENSOR}} DNA test only. The customer receives the Beauty report; cosmetic products are not included (those require a Beauty+ subscription or standalone {{COSMETICS_DAY_NIGHT_95ML}} / {{COSMETICS_BODY_LOTION_235ML}} subscription).

## Subscription packages — the "+" line

### {{PACKAGE_SHAPE_PLUS}} — Shape+ (subscription)

A subscription bundle: free {{WEIGHT_SENSOR}} DNA test plus a {{SUPPLEMENT_WEIGHT}} subscription. Minimum subscription term 12 months. The Shape DNA test is included free during the minimum term.

### {{PACKAGE_LIFESTYLE_PLUS}} — Lifestyle+ (subscription, primary pitch)

The flagship subscription product: free Lifestyle DNA bundle ({{WEIGHT_SENSOR}}, {{NUTRITION_SENSOR}}, {{TOXO_SENSOR}}, {{BURNOUT_SENSOR}}, {{BIOLOGICAL_AGE_SENSOR}}, {{RECIPE_BOOK}}) plus {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}} subscription. Minimum term 12 months. The full Lifestyle DNA test and the printed report are included free during the minimum term.

### {{PACKAGE_PERFORMANCE_PLUS}} — Performance+ (subscription)

The Performance subscription bundle: free Performance DNA test plus {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}} subscription. Minimum term 15 months — longer than Shape+ and Lifestyle+ because of the higher athletic-load formulation work.

### {{PACKAGE_BEAUTY_PLUS}} — Beauty+ (subscription)

A subscription bundle: free {{BEAUTY_SENSOR}} DNA test plus {{COSMETICS_DAY_NIGHT_95ML}} (DAY + NIGHT serum) subscription. Minimum term 12 months.

## Subscription mechanics

### Billing rhythm

All "+" subscriptions bill monthly. The supplement or cosmetic shipment is produced and shipped in 3-month packs every three months — so the customer always has uninterrupted daily supply while paying monthly.

### Cancellation and renewal

After the minimum term ends, subscriptions auto-renew in 3-month cycles. Cancellation is possible online at any time after the minimum term. Pausing is also possible online.

### Free DNA test rule

For "+" subscriptions, the matching DNA test is included free during the minimum subscription period. If the customer cancels before the minimum term ends, the value of the DNA test may become payable — exact terms per the reseller's published commercial rules.
