## Overview

### What the customer receives

The {{BEAUTY_SENSOR}} report is a roughly 60-page personalised document covering 20+ skin-related genes: collagen turnover, UV defence, hydration, oxidative stress at the skin level, biological skin age, inflammation tendency, and the nutrient and cosmetic recommendations that follow. Feeds directly into personalised {{COSMETICS_DAY_NIGHT_95ML}} / {{COSMETICS_BODY_LOTION_235ML}} formulations.

### Tone and framing

Recommendations are written in the second person and framed as "where your skin needs extra support" rather than aesthetic judgements. The report distinguishes between intrinsic ageing (genetic baseline) and extrinsic ageing (modifiable through sun protection, nutrition, lifestyle).

## Report sections

### Front matter

Foreword, brief intro to skin biology, the standard disclaimer.

### Results overview

A "Your results at a glance" summary.

### Topic chapters

Collagen breakdown (MMP1), collagen production (caffeine clearance via CYP1A2 + prolidase pathway), UV protection (MC1R variants), skin hydration (HA pathway, UVB-driven HA loss), oxidative stress at the skin level (GST family, SOD2, GPX1), CoQ10 activation in skin (NQO1), selenium requirement (GPX1), inflammation tendency (TNFA, IL6, IL1A, IL1B), and biological skin age (TERT, BICD1, PPARG).

### Closing pages

About-the-laboratory page, technical details, and an appendix mapping each finding to the matching personalised-cosmetic ingredient stack.

## Chapter structure

### How every chapter is built

Scientific intro, "most important details at a glance" box, what the gene does at the skin level, customer's genotype + verdict, recommendation paragraph (dietary + topical), PubMed citations.

### Verdict categories

Three frames: "favourable" (preservation strategy), "moderate variant" (targeted support), "high-impact variant" (active intervention strategy).

## Example findings

### Example 1 — favourable, preservation

Topic: Collagen breakdown. Gene: MMP1 (1G/1G). Verdict: "Low-expression promoter — favourable collagen-retention profile." Narrative: lower constitutive MMP-1 expression; preserve through sun protection and standard antioxidant support.

### Example 2 — moderate, targeted topical

Topic: UV protection. Gene: MC1R (one reduced-function variant). Verdict: "Photoprotection moderately reduced." Narrative: lower eumelanin synthesis means more UV reaches the dermis. Recommendation: daily broad-spectrum SPF + topical C + E (+ ferulic acid) serum.

### Example 3 — high-impact, active intervention

Topic: Collagen breakdown. Gene: MMP1 (2G/2G). Verdict: "High-expression promoter — collagenolysis amplified under stress." Narrative: 2G/2G means inducible MMP-1 is amplified by UV, oxidative stress, and inflammation. Recommendation: stack lutein, vitamin C (collagen synthesis), vitamin E, phytosterols in cosmetics; strict sun protection.

### Example 4 — high-impact, hydration

Topic: Skin hydration. Genes: MC1R reduced-function + UVB-driven HA loss. Verdict: "Hydration vulnerability elevated." Narrative: weaker UV defence + accelerated dermal HA loss = drier mature skin trajectory. Recommendation: rigorous SPF + topical hyaluronic acid (mixed molecular weights) + oral HA.

### Example 5 — pro-inflammatory genotype

Topic: Inflammation. Genes: TNFA + IL6 + CRP pro-inflammatory variants. Verdict: "Inflammation-driven photoaging risk elevated." Narrative: exaggerated inflammatory response to UV → faster transition from acute photodamage to chronic photoaging. Recommendation: omega-3 (EPA/DHA), MSM, resveratrol, vitamin D, vitamin C, zinc; reinforced sun protection.
