## Overview

### What is {{BURNOUT_SENSOR}}?

{{BURNOUT_SENSOR}} examines genes related to stress resilience, with COMT as the central gene. The COMT enzyme degrades neurotransmitters such as dopamine and noradrenaline; variants influence how quickly stress hormones are cleared, affecting baseline stress tolerance.

### Who is {{BURNOUT_SENSOR}} for?

People in high-pressure professional roles, individuals who recover slowly from stressful events, people interested in understanding their personal stress profile, or anyone integrating stress management into a broader prevention strategy.

## What is analyzed

### Topics covered by {{BURNOUT_SENSOR}}

COMT gene activity (stress hormone clearance rate), stress-buffering micronutrient needs, and downstream impact on sleep and recovery.

## Deliverables

### What the customer receives

A focused report (~40 pages) explaining the COMT variant, its practical implications, and recommendations. Findings feed into {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}} dosing for B-vitamins, magnesium, and other stress-supportive nutrients.

### Sample requirement

A single saliva sample collected at home using {{COMPANY_NAME}}'s proprietary saliva device.
