## Overview

### What is {{TOXO_SENSOR}}?

{{TOXO_SENSOR}} examines genes responsible for detoxification — particularly the Phase II glutathione S-transferase system (GSTM1, GSTP1, GSTT1). The analysis reveals how effectively the body neutralises burned substances, industrial chemicals, heavy metals, and free radicals.

### Who is {{TOXO_SENSOR}} for?

Smokers, frequent grillers, people exposed to chemicals professionally, fish and shellfish lovers, people with amalgam dental fillings, regular coffee or alcohol drinkers, and anyone interested in oxidative-stress reduction and anti-ageing.

## What is analyzed

### Topics covered by {{TOXO_SENSOR}}

Burned substances (smoke, grilled food), industrial chemicals and pesticides, heavy metals (fish, shellfish, amalgam), free radicals and ageing, caffeine metabolism, and genetic alcoholism risk indicators.

### How it relates to other analyses

Phase II enzymes are also partially analysed in {{PHARMACO_SENSOR}} (TPMT, NAT2, UGT1A1). Phase I CYP enzymes are covered in {{PHARMACO_SENSOR}}. The two analyses are complementary rather than overlapping.

## Deliverables

### What the customer receives

A ~70-page personalised report explaining each detoxification gene, its variant in the customer's profile, and practical implications. Recommendations feed into {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}} if ordered (e.g. adjusted antioxidant dosing).

### Sample requirement

A single saliva sample collected at home using {{COMPANY_NAME}}'s proprietary saliva device.
