## What is collected

### Sample material

A saliva sample collected at home using {{COMPANY_NAME}}'s proprietary collection device. The sample is sent to the laboratory in Eugendorf, Salzburg, Austria.

### Personal data

Identity (name, date of birth), shipping address, contact email, order data, payment data (handled via the payment processor, not stored by {{COMPANY_NAME}}), and consent records.

### Laboratory data

DNA-derived data covering the variants relevant to the analyses the customer ordered, lifestyle questionnaire responses, and (if applicable) blood-test results.

---

## How data is handled at the lab

### Identity decoupled during analysis

During laboratory analysis, the sample is identified only by barcode. The laboratory staff do not see the customer's identity. Only after analysis is complete is the result linked back to the customer's account.

### DNA destruction after analysis

The DNA itself is destroyed after analysis. No further analyses can be performed on it without a new sample. This is a structural privacy measure, not just a policy.

### Retention of data vs sample

Sample = destroyed after analysis. Data (identity, order, analysis result) = retained under standard medical-data protection rules. The distinction matters: the genetic material is gone; the analysis report is kept so the customer can access it via the {{REPORT_PORTAL}}.

---

## Use of data

### Use limited to the customer's order

DNA-derived data is used only for the analyses the customer ordered. Adding new analyses later is possible because the original data is retained; running unrelated analyses without consent is not.

### No commercial data sharing

Data is not shared for commercial purposes. It is not sold to research bodies. It is not shared with marketing partners. Pharmaceutical companies do not receive customer data.

### Reseller access

The reseller through which the customer placed their order can see order-related data needed to support the customer (e.g. status, shipping). The reseller does not see raw genetic data unless the customer explicitly chooses to share their report.

## Customer rights

### Access

Customers can access their analysis reports at any time via the {{REPORT_PORTAL}}. They can also request a copy of all personal data held about them under GDPR.

### Correction

Customers can correct identity or contact information via the {{REPORT_PORTAL}} or the reseller's support channel.

### Deletion

Customers can request deletion of their account and personal data. The DNA is already destroyed after analysis. Order and identity data are retained only as required by applicable legal data-retention rules (typically 7-10 years for financial records); outside those, deletion is honoured on request.

### Portability

Customers can request their data in a portable format. The DNA-derived data file (variants relevant to the analyses purchased) can be exported.

### Withdrawal of consent

Customers can withdraw consent for future processing at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing that already happened under prior consent.

## GDPR Specifics

### Legal basis

Processing is based on the customer's explicit consent at order time, plus contract performance (delivering the ordered analyses). Special-category data (genetic data is special-category under GDPR Art. 9) is processed only with explicit consent.

### Data controller

The reseller through whom the customer placed the order is typically the data controller for customer-facing data. Novogenia GmbH is the data processor for laboratory analysis. Specific roles can vary by reseller — see the reseller's privacy policy.

### Data location

Personal data and laboratory data are processed and stored within the European Union (primarily Austria). No transfer to non-EU jurisdictions is part of the standard workflow.

### Sub-processors

Any sub-processors (payment, shipping, IT infrastructure) used to fulfil the order are listed in the reseller's published sub-processor list. All sub-processors are bound by GDPR-compliant contracts.

## Where to ask questions

### Customer support route

For privacy questions, customers should contact the reseller's customer support channel. The reseller will route privacy-officer inquiries appropriately. For inquiries that need the laboratory's input, the reseller forwards to Novogenia.
