## Subscription Mechanics

### How does the subscription and delivery process work?

When a subscription including a lab analysis is ordered: the first monthly installment is charged at order time, and a sample kit is sent to the customer. The customer collects their saliva sample and sends it back. The lab performs the analysis, calculates the recipe, produces the reports and the first 3-month supply of {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}}, and ships everything. From that point, monthly billing continues. The supplement is produced and shipped in 3-month packs at 3-month intervals, so the customer has uninterrupted daily supply while paying in monthly installments.
