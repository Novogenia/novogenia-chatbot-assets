## Subscription Cancellation

### How do I cancel a "+" subscription?

Subscriptions on the "+" line ({{PACKAGE_LIFESTYLE_PLUS}}, {{PACKAGE_SHAPE_PLUS}}, {{PACKAGE_PERFORMANCE_PLUS}}, {{PACKAGE_BEAUTY_PLUS}}) can be cancelled online at any time after the minimum subscription term has ended. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current 3-month delivery cycle, so the customer still receives the supplement supply they have already paid for.

### What is the minimum term for each subscription?

{{PACKAGE_LIFESTYLE_PLUS}} and {{PACKAGE_SHAPE_PLUS}} require a 12-month minimum subscription. {{PACKAGE_PERFORMANCE_PLUS}} requires a 15-month minimum (because of the higher athletic-load formulation work involved). {{PACKAGE_BEAUTY_PLUS}} requires a 12-month minimum. Standalone supplement and cosmetic subscriptions ({{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}}+, {{SUPPLEMENT_WEIGHT}}+, {{COSMETICS_DAY_NIGHT_95ML}}+, {{COSMETICS_BODY_LOTION_235ML}}+) operate in 3-month cycles and can be cancelled at the end of any 3-month period.

### What happens if I cancel before the minimum term ends?

The DNA test that came free with the "+" subscription is included free during the minimum term. Cancellation before the term ends may result in the value of the included DNA test becoming payable. The exact terms are published in the reseller's commercial rules and shown at checkout.

### Auto-renewal after the minimum term

After the minimum term ends, subscriptions automatically renew in 3-month cycles unless cancelled. The customer is not locked in beyond the minimum term — only the next 3-month cycle is committed at any point.

## Subscription Pausing

### Can I pause my subscription?

Yes. The subscription can be paused at any time online. While paused, no further shipments are produced and no further billing occurs. The customer can resume whenever they are ready.

### What happens to my data while paused?

The customer's analysis results, formulation, and account data are preserved during a pause. When the subscription resumes, the next shipment uses the latest formulation including any algorithm improvements introduced during the pause.

## One-time Order Refunds

### Can I return a DNA test if I haven't sent the sample?

Yes. If the saliva kit has not been used (sample not submitted to the lab), the order can be cancelled and refunded within the statutory cooling-off period applicable in the customer's country.

### Can I return a DNA test after sample submission?

Once a sample has been submitted to the laboratory, analysis costs have been incurred and the product is considered personalised. Refunds are generally not possible after sample submission, except in cases of laboratory failure to deliver a valid analysis.

### Can I return a supplement or cosmetic?

Personalised supplements and cosmetics are made-to-order for the individual customer and are excluded from standard return rights under EU consumer law (personalised products exception). They cannot be returned for refund once produced.

## Quality & Logistics Issues

### What if my sample fails analysis?

The laboratory can attempt up to three independent analyses on the same saliva sample. If all three fail, a new sample kit is sent free of charge. No additional charge is applied for the re-collection.

### What if my package is lost or damaged in shipping?

If a shipment is lost or arrives damaged, the customer should contact the reseller's customer support. Replacement is arranged at no additional charge once the issue is verified.

### What if the laboratory cannot deliver a result?

If, after all attempts, the laboratory cannot produce a valid analysis from the customer's sample, the analysis fee is refunded. Any associated supplement subscription is also paused until a valid analysis is available.

## Data and Account Closure

### How do I delete my account and data?

Customer account closure and data deletion are handled via the reseller's customer support channel. The DNA itself is already destroyed after the original analysis is complete. Identity, order, and result data are retained only as required by applicable legal data-retention rules; outside those, deletion is honoured on request.
