## Identity & Vision

### What is Darwin AG?

Darwin AG is a publicly listed German biotechnology holding company headquartered in Munich. It owns and operates a portfolio of biotech subsidiaries and minority investments centered on personalized health products, services, and therapies based on individual biological data. Novogenia GmbH (Salzburg) is the group's flagship operating company. Darwin AG was founded in 2021 and listed on the Munich Stock Exchange (m:access / XETRA) the same year.

### What is the "Analyze. Learn. Solve. Explore." framework?

Darwin's strategic framework has four pillars. Analyze: laboratory analysis of human genome and metabolome data. Learn: AI-powered interpretation of millions of scientific publications and lab results. Solve: personalized supplements, cosmetics, reports, and therapies derived from each individual's data. Explore: applying the same principle to new biotech areas through acquisitions, in-house startups, and clinical studies.

### What is Darwin's mission?

Darwin's mission is to deliver personalized products, services, and therapies based on each individual's unique biological data. The group combines advances in biotechnology, genetics, and AI to move the healthcare industry from one-size-fits-all towards data-driven personalization at industrial scale.

### What is Darwin's long-term vision?

CEO Dr. Daniel Wallerstorfer's vision extends beyond today's nutrigenetics into therapy optimization, gene correction, lifespan extension, and engineered biology. Darwin invests across the spectrum from lifestyle genetics to advanced gene therapy (via minority stakes such as Nephrogen) so the group is positioned wherever personalized biology becomes commercially viable.

### Planned rename to Novogenia AG in 2026

Darwin AG is planned to be renamed to Novogenia AG during 2026. The rename aligns the parent company name with its flagship operating subsidiary Novogenia GmbH, which is the source of most group revenue and the public-facing brand familiar to partners and end customers. Until the rename takes legal effect, "Darwin AG" remains the correct corporate name on all official documents, stock listings, and contracts.

## Headquarters & Corporate Details

Darwin AG's registered office is at Brienner Straße 7, 80333 Munich, Germany. The Munich seat is the legal and investor-relations base of the holding company; operating activities take place at the subsidiaries' locations, primarily Eugendorf near Salzburg, Austria.

## Leadership & Governance

### Who is the CEO of Darwin AG?

Dr. Daniel Wallerstorfer is the founder and Chief Executive Officer of Darwin AG. He holds a BSc in molecular biology and a PhD in biotechnology from the University of Manchester. He is also founder and CEO of Novogenia GmbH and focuses his time on innovation, product development, scaling, technology, AI models, and group strategy. ## The Three Divisions

### Diagnostics & Personalized Products division

This is Darwin's largest division and the source of most group revenue. It comprises Novogenia GmbH (lab plus supplement and cosmetics manufacturing), Novogenia Marketing GmbH (small-reseller channel under the NovoDaily brand), HLN Genetik (clinical and IVF genetics in Vienna), Mendelio (in-house software development), Genius (pharmacy channel), and NovoMedic (medical-professional channel).

### Drug Import & Medical Devices division

This division consists of Darwin Pharma Holding GmbH (Munich) and its subsidiaries — Medicopharm AG, INOPHA GmbH, ChiruTec GmbH — plus 305 Care GmbH. These companies were acquired for their profitability and their established access to pharmacies and the public health system, which are strategic distribution channels for the Diagnostics & Personalized Products division.

### Minority Investments division

This division holds non-controlling stakes (under 10%) in selected biotech companies that extend Darwin's coverage of the field without requiring full integration. Current holdings include Nephrogen Inc. (gene therapy, US), GenomeLink (bioinformatics, US — held indirectly via Novogenia), Spot Health Inc. (lab logistics, US), and GenePlanet d.o.o. (genetics, Slovenia).

## Controlled Subsidiaries

### Novogenia GmbH (100%)

Novogenia GmbH in Eugendorf near Salzburg is Darwin's flagship operating company and is wholly owned by Darwin AG. It runs the genetic laboratory, supplement manufacturing, and cosmetics production that underpin the group's core product lines, and supplies the analyses and personalized products distributed by other group companies and external partners.

### Novogenia Marketing GmbH (100%)

Novogenia Marketing GmbH, also based in Eugendorf, is wholly owned by Darwin AG. It operates the NovoDaily brand — Novogenia's own brand for small resellers who want to sell Novogenia products without building their own label. It is not a direct-to-consumer operation.

### HLN Genetik GmbH (70%)

Darwin acquired the controlling majority of HLN Genetik in 2022. Headquartered in Vienna, HLN Genetik holds the exclusive Austrian licence for preimplantation genetic testing (used in IVF) and an extensive licence for medical diagnostic testing across known genetic diseases. It anchors the group's high-end medical genetics offering.

### Mendelio Srl (85%)

Mendelio is Darwin's in-house software-development arm, based in Iași, Romania. The team builds proprietary software and AI engines for all Darwin-group companies and selected external partners. All software developed by Mendelio is intellectual property of the Darwin group.

### NovoMedic GmbH (85%)

NovoMedic is a Darwin in-house startup based in Eugendorf, Salzburg, that brings personalized supplement solutions to medical professionals. Clinicians can offer Novogenia blood and DNA tests to patients, receive a recommended nutrient recipe, and adapt it with their expertise. NovoMedic reports working with 500+ doctors.

### Genius GmbH (67.5%)

Genius is a Darwin startup, run together with Novogenia, that brings personalized health solutions to pharmacies. Through geniusdnahub.com, partner pharmacies can offer genetic diagnostics, individually tailored micronutrients, and customised cosmetics, positioning the pharmacy as a center of modern preventive healthcare.

### 305 Care GmbH (70%)

305 Care GmbH, based in Düsseldorf, Germany, sits within the Diagnostics & Personalized Products division and contributes to Darwin's German-market presence in personalized health services.

### Darwin Pharma Holding GmbH (100%)

Darwin Pharma Holding GmbH in Munich is the wholly owned holding entity for Darwin's Drug Import & Medical Devices division. Through it, Darwin owns Medicopharm AG (100%, Nußdorf am Inn), INOPHA GmbH (51%, Ludwigsfelde), and ChiruTec GmbH (51%, Ludwigsfelde) — companies focused on drug import and distribution and medical devices.

### Darwin Immobilien GmbH (100%)

Darwin Immobilien GmbH in Eugendorf is a wholly owned property-holding subsidiary that manages real-estate assets associated with the group's Salzburg-area facilities, including land and buildings related to the planned new headquarters.

## Minority Investments

### Nephrogen Inc.

Nephrogen is a US-based pioneer in gene therapy for genetic kidney disease, with Polycystic Kidney Disease (PKD) as its first target. The startup is enrolled in the Y Combinator accelerator. Darwin's minority stake represents an early position in actual cures for genetic diseases — an inevitable future direction in medicine.

### GenomeLink Inc.

GenomeLink is a US-based bioinformatics company that lets B2C customers upload their existing DNA data (for example from 23andMe) to gain additional insights into health, traits, and ancestry. Darwin holds non-controlling shares indirectly through Novogenia GmbH.

### Spot Health Inc. (SpotKits)

SpotKits, based in Cincinnati, USA, is a B2B service provider that handles branded sample-kit delivery to end customers, sample transport to laboratories, and result submission back to B2B partners. With rapid growth, the company is investing in an in-house blood-analysis lab.

### GenePlanet d.o.o.

GenePlanet, based in Ljubljana, Slovenia, is a personal-genetics company in the Darwin minority-investment portfolio. The investment broadens Darwin's exposure to European personal-genetics distribution.

Genius (geniusdnahub.com) is the pharmacy-facing brand of the joint Genius–Novogenia offering. It packages genetic diagnostics, personalized micronutrients, and customised cosmetics into a turnkey service for partner pharmacies.

## Technology Platforms

### Genetic laboratory platform

Darwin operates fully licensed laboratories that cover the entire spectrum of genetic and metabolomic testing, from lifestyle personal genomics to embryo screening for IVF. Custom microarray technology coupled with AI analyses up to 70 million genetic variations per individual sample.

### Personalized supplementation platform

Darwin developed proprietary technology to produce unique vitamin and mineral mixtures for each individual based on their lab analysis data. The microtransporter (pellet) format enables milligram-level dosing precision, gradual all-day release, and spatial separation of nutrients that would otherwise compete for absorption — personalization down to the individual at industrial scale.

### Personalized cosmetics platform

Darwin's in-house cosmetics platform produces unique cosmetic formulations based on each individual's genetic and lab-analysis data. With over 30 billion possible recipes, every product is labelled with the customer's name and is effectively unique.

### Artificial Intelligence (Deep Genome AI)

Darwin's Deep Genome AI engine interprets genetic data, identifies trait patterns, decodes millions of scientific publications, and continuously feeds the group's product personalization pipeline. AI is also used in an "analyse → product → re-analyse → adapt product" feedback loop that improves recommendations over time.

## Sales Channels

### Small resellers (own-brand NovoDaily)

Darwin and Novogenia do not sell direct to consumers. For small resellers who want to distribute Novogenia products without building their own brand, the group offers its own NovoDaily brand as a turnkey channel — at-home test kits plus personalized supplement subscriptions ready to resell.

### Pharmacies and retail (B2B2C)

Pharmacies and retailers act as a B2B2C channel, selling Darwin-group products under their own brands or partner brands. The Genius offering and the Drug Import & Medical Devices division both reinforce this channel.

### Doctors and clinics (B2B2C)

Doctors, clinics, and IVF centres receive Darwin services via NovoMedic and HLN Genetik. They integrate Darwin diagnostics and personalized supplements into patient care, with Darwin handling lab work and supplement production behind the scenes.

### Companies (B2B2C)

Corporate partners — including large reseller groups, supplement companies, and beauty brands — license Darwin's analyses and personalized products under their own labels. This partnership model is the backbone of Novogenia's reseller channel.

### Therapists and beauty professionals (B2B2C)

Independent therapists, beauty professionals, gyms, and nutritionists distribute Darwin products to their clients, often as add-on services that complement their core offering.

## Production Capacity & Expansion Plan

### Current and target capacities

The current Salzburg facility supports approximately 81,000 supplement packs and 27,000 cosmetics packs in production, with up to 280,000 daily DNA-test capacity. After the planned headquarters expansion, the maximum-possible capacity grows to roughly 2.5 million supplement packs (€450M revenue), 324,000 cosmetics packs (€58M), and 2.8 million DNA tests (€700M) per period.

### New headquarters plan

A new headquarters has been commissioned at the existing Salzburg site. Construction plans were submitted and were awaiting the building permit at the time of the January 2026 company presentation. Construction is expected to start in Q1 2026 and to take approximately 1.5 years, after which production space triples and laboratory space grows fivefold.

### Long-term revenue capacity

Once the expansion is complete, the integrated Salzburg site is designed to support cumulative annual revenue capacity well above €1 billion across the three core product lines (DNA tests, supplements, cosmetics), enabling Darwin to scale its existing partner network without becoming production-constrained.

## Group Financials at a Glance

### Cumulative revenue

Darwin's group companies have generated approximately €0.5 billion in cumulative revenue since inception. The COVID-19 pandemic was a major contributor to this total, with €110.9 million of group revenue in 2021 and €210.3 million in 2022 driven by Novogenia's Austrian SARS-CoV-2 testing operations.

### Post-COVID revenue trajectory

Group revenue contracted sharply once COVID testing wound down: €18.2 million in 2023 (a deliberate transition year), then €25.3 million in 2024 (+39%), and a forecast of €56.0 million for 2025 (+117%). The 2024-to-2025 doubling reflects scaling of the core diagnostics and personalized-products business.

### Cash position

Darwin reports approximately €54 million in cash reserves. The strong cash position funds the planned headquarters expansion, ongoing scalability investments, and selective acquisitions and minority investments without requiring additional capital raises.

### Material costs and margins

Material costs vary by product line: roughly 20% of revenue for DNA tests (80% Contribution Margin 1), 15% for supplements (85% CM1), and 6% for cosmetics (94% CM1). At the group level, material costs ran 50–66% of revenue depending on year, reflecting product mix.

### Growth indicators

Order growth in the core business is reported at 18% per month, and Darwin describes itself as growing roughly 100% per year. The publicly reported 2024-to-2025 group revenue growth of +117% is consistent with these operational indicators.

## Dividend Policy & History

### Dividend strategy

Darwin AG follows a dividend strategy: as long as company development is not impeded, it aims to pay dividends to shareholders annually. Dividend amounts are proposed by the Executive Board and approved by the Annual General Meeting.

### Dividend history

Dividends paid per share since listing: €0.44 for 2021, €1.75 for 2022 (a one-off COVID bonus reflecting that year's exceptional results), €0.56 for 2023, and €0.56 for 2024.

## Global Presence

### Austria — operating heart of the group

Darwin's main operating presence is in Austria. Eugendorf near Salzburg hosts Novogenia GmbH, Novogenia Marketing GmbH, NovoMedic GmbH, Genius GmbH, and Darwin Immobilien GmbH. Vienna hosts HLN Genetik GmbH. Most laboratory work, supplement production, and cosmetics production take place at the Salzburg site.

### Germany — holding and pharma division

Germany hosts the holding company Darwin AG (Munich) and the Drug Import & Medical Devices division: Darwin Pharma Holding GmbH (Munich), Medicopharm AG (Nußdorf am Inn), INOPHA GmbH and ChiruTec GmbH (both Ludwigsfelde), and 305 Care GmbH (Düsseldorf).

### Romania — software development

Mendelio Srl in Iași, Romania, is Darwin's in-house software and AI engineering centre. All proprietary software for the group, including the Deep Genome AI engine, is developed here.

### United States — minority investments

In the United States, Darwin holds minority stakes in Nephrogen Inc. (New York, gene therapy), GenomeLink Inc. (Berkeley, bioinformatics), and Spot Health Inc. / SpotKits (Cincinnati, lab logistics).

### Slovenia — minority investment

Darwin holds a minority investment in GenePlanet d.o.o. (Ljubljana, Slovenia), broadening exposure to personal-genetics distribution in Central and Southeast Europe.

### United Arab Emirates — national project

Through the exclusive partnership with G42, Darwin has a strategic operational footprint in the UAE focused on the 1-million-genome national project. The UAE engagement does not at present involve a Darwin legal entity but represents a major commercial and scientific commitment.
