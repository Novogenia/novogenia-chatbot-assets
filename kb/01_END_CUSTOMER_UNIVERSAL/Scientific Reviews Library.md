## Nutrition genetics reviews

### Caffeine and CYP1A2

Reviews the evidence that the CYP1A2 rs762551 polymorphism splits the population into fast and slow caffeine metabolisers, with downstream consequences for hypertension and cardiovascular risk. Backs the caffeine-restriction recommendation given to slow-metaboliser customers in {{NUTRITION_SENSOR}}.

### HDL cholesterol and Omega-3 (APOA1)

Reviews evidence that the APOA1 rs670 variant flips the usual benefit of Omega-3 on HDL cholesterol — for carriers, Omega-3 supplementation lowers HDL rather than raises it. Underpins the APOA1-specific recommendation to substitute phytosterols for Omega-3 in {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}}.

### Folic acid and MTHFR

Reviews the MTHFR C677T and A1298C variants, the rate-limiting step of folic-acid activation, and the rationale for direct methylfolate supplementation rather than standard folic acid in impaired carriers.

### Homocysteine and the methylation cycle

Reviews MTHFR, MTRR and MTR polymorphisms in homocysteine regulation. Methionine remethylation, cardiovascular risk, and how the methylation cycle integrates folic acid, B12, B2, and B6.

### Coenzyme Q10 and NQO1

Reviews the NQO1 Pro187Ser polymorphism and its effect on conversion of CoQ10 to its active form ubiquinol. Carriers benefit from ubiquinol or higher CoQ10 doses.

### Detoxification

Reviews the evidence base for genotype-driven detoxification recommendations. Phase-I and phase-II enzyme variants (CYP family, GST family, NAT2) and their interaction with carcinogen exposure.

### Industrial chemicals and heavy metals (GSTT1, GSTM1)

Reviews the GSTT1 and GSTM1 null alleles in detoxification of chemicals and heavy metals — the genetic basis for personalised toxin-avoidance recommendations.

### Polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (CYP1A1, CYP1B1)

Reviews CYP1A1 and CYP1B1 polymorphisms in detoxification of grilled/smoked food carcinogens and the resulting lung-cancer-risk modulation.

### Free radicals and oxidative stress

Reviews the genes involved in free-radical neutralisation (SOD2, GPX1, CAT, NQO1) and how individual antioxidant needs vary with genotype.

### Selenium and GPX1

Reviews the GPX1 Pro198Leu polymorphism in glutathione peroxidase activity and the per-person selenium requirement that follows from it.

### Salt sensitivity (AGT, GNB3)

Reviews AGT rs699 and GNB3 rs5443 in salt sensitivity and blood-pressure regulation. Backs the personalised sodium recommendation in {{NUTRITION_SENSOR}}.

### Vitamin D and VDR

Reviews VDR polymorphisms and their effect on the biological action of Vitamin D3 at the receptor level. The basis for the personalised Vitamin D dose given in {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}}.

### Lactose tolerance (LCT)

Reviews the LCT rs4988235 polymorphism and lactase persistence — the genetic basis for adult lactose digestion and the implication for calcium nutrition in lactose-intolerant customers.

### Iron metabolism (HFE)

Reviews HFE rs1800562, rs1800730 and rs1799945 polymorphisms in iron metabolism and the elevated hereditary hemochromatosis risk. The basis for the iron-restriction recommendation in HFE-variant carriers.

### Immune system

Reviews IL6, CRP, TNFA and IL1RN polymorphisms in chronic inflammation and immune response. Underpins the inflammation-modulating nutrient recommendations in {{NUTRITION_SENSOR}} and {{BLOOD_PANEL_INFLAMMATION}} interpretation.

### Triglycerides (APOA5)

Reviews APOA5 rs662799 in postprandial triglyceride regulation and cardiovascular risk.

### LDL cholesterol (APOB, SREBP2, IL6R)

Reviews APOB, SREBP2 and IL6R polymorphisms in LDL-cholesterol regulation and the spectrum from familial to polygenic hypercholesterolaemia.

### COMT and neurotransmitter methylation

Reviews COMT Val158Met in catecholamine and oestrogen methylation — relevant to {{BURNOUT_SENSOR}}, stress response, and hormone metabolism.

### AHCY and methylation balance

Reviews AHCY polymorphisms and the breakdown of S-adenosylhomocysteine to homocysteine. Determines methylation capacity at the cell level.

### MTRR cobalamin recycling

Reviews MTRR rs1801394 A66G in methionine synthase reactivation and B12 recycling.

### MTR methionine synthase

Reviews MTR polymorphisms in homocysteine remethylation to methionine.

### Integrated methylation cycle

A consolidated review of the full one-carbon-metabolism methylation cycle — MTHFR, MTR, MTRR, AHCY and COMT polymorphisms — and how they interact across nutrient need, neurotransmitter balance, and cardiovascular risk.

## How the bot should use this library

### Citing a review

When a customer asks "is this scientifically backed?" or "where does this come from?", the bot can name the specific review (e.g. "There's an internal review on APOA1 rs670 and Omega-3 / HDL that summarises around X studies") and offer to share it or summarise the key finding.

### Linking to genes

Where a customer's question relates to a specific gene (CYP1A2, MTHFR, VDR, APOA1, FTO, etc.), the bot can refer to the gene-specific review in this library. Each review consolidates the per-gene evidence and is the most defensible reference {{COMPANY_NAME}} can offer.

Reviews are mapped to products in the section headings above. Use this map to pull the right review when discussing a specific {{COMPANY_NAME}} product claim.
