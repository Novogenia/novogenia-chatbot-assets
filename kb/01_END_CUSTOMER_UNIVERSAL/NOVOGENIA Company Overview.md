## Identity & Founding

### What is Novogenia?

Novogenia is an Austrian biotech company specializing in preventive genetic testing, personalized nutrition, supplements, and cosmetics. Since its founding in 2009, Novogenia has built a fully integrated platform that combines its own genetic laboratory, supplement production, and cosmetics manufacturing under one roof. The company partners with health and lifestyle professionals worldwide to deliver scientifically backed genetic analyses that form the basis for personalized recommendations.

### Where is Novogenia headquartered?

Novogenia is headquartered in Eugendorf, near Salzburg, Austria. The site combines a 4,000 m² laboratory and production facility — housing genetic analytics, supplement manufacturing, cosmetics production, and administration in one integrated location. All laboratory work and product manufacturing take place in Austria under "Made in Austria" quality standards.

### When was Novogenia founded and by whom?

Novogenia was founded in 2009 by Dr. Daniel Wallerstorfer, a molecular biologist, epigeneticist, and biotechnologist who continues to lead the company as Chief Executive Officer. The company has been profitable since 2013 and has grown into one of Europe's leading providers of nutrigenetics and preventive genetic testing.

### What is Novogenia's mission?

Novogenia's mission is to empower people to take charge of their health and well-being through personalized analyses and products based on their individual genetic and biological data. Internally, the company follows an "MVP first, then quality and perfection" principle — learning through implementation, investing in internal talent before external growth, and operating with transparency, openness, and trust to deliver the best possible customer experience.

### What is Novogenia's vision?

Novogenia's vision is to become the leading provider of personalized products and services based on individual biological data — first in Europe, then worldwide. The company focuses on high-quality, scientifically backed solutions rather than the simplest or cheapest options. Novogenia combines start-up speed and flexibility with the strength of a market leader, and considers its growth inseparably linked to the success of its partner network.

## Key Figures & Scale

### How many employees does Novogenia have?

Novogenia employs approximately 85 people today across its laboratory, supplement and cosmetics production, and administration functions, all based at the Salzburg headquarters.

### How large is Novogenia's facility?

Novogenia operates a single 4,000 m² site that integrates the genetic laboratory, supplement production, cosmetics production, and administrative offices. Concentrating all functions on one campus enables tight quality control and rapid coordination between analysis and manufacturing teams.

### How large is Novogenia's partner network?

Novogenia works with over 3,000 partners in more than 30 countries. Partners include nutritionists, fitness trainers, health practitioners, physiotherapists, pharmacies, gyms, and other professionals who use Novogenia's genetic analyses and personalized products as part of their own services.

### What is Novogenia's daily laboratory capacity?

Novogenia's laboratory is built for high-throughput operation, with capacity for up to 275,000 samples per day. This capacity was demonstrated during the COVID-19 pandemic, when Novogenia operated as Austria's largest COVID-19 testing laboratory at peak demand.

### How many PCR analyses has Novogenia performed?

Since 2009, Novogenia has performed more than 35 million PCR analyses. This volume reflects both the company's pandemic-era COVID testing operations and its ongoing routine processing of genetic samples for preventive and lifestyle analyses.

### How many genetic variations are tested per sample?

Each Novogenia sample is analyzed for more than 70 million genetic variations. This is achieved by combining a custom-designed DNA microarray with AI-based bioinformatics that expands the directly measured variants into a much broader dataset — providing one of the most extensive genetic readouts in the industry.

### What is Novogenia's revenue track record?

Novogenia has been profitable since 2013 and has generated approximately half a billion euros in cumulative revenue since inception. Revenue is diversified across the reseller channel, contract manufacturing, OEM production, and the recurring 90-day subscription model for personalized supplements.

## Leadership Team

### Who is the CEO of Novogenia?

Dr. Daniel Wallerstorfer is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Novogenia. A molecular biologist, epigeneticist, and biotechnologist by training, he is also the author of the book "Die Macht unserer Gene" ("The Power of our Genes" — English edition "Unlock Your Genes for Precision Health"). Under his leadership, Novogenia has grown from a start-up into a European market leader in nutrigenetics and preventive genetic testing.

### What is Dr. Wallerstorfer's academic background?

Dr. Wallerstorfer studied at the University of Manchester, completing his Bachelor's degree in Molecular Biology followed by a PhD in Biotechnology. During his PhD his supervisor Professor Alan Dickson supported the early Novogenia work, including allowing Dr. Wallerstorfer to install his first real-time PCR cycler in the university lab where the first genetic tests were developed.

### How did Novogenia start?

While at Manchester, Dr. Wallerstorfer entered the University's Business Plan Competition together with his colleague Jacky Wu, with a plan for direct-to-consumer genetic testing for disease prevention. They placed third — a result that produced a clear roadmap for the company. Initial seed funding came from a private investor, which funded the first real-time PCR equipment and the first generation of tests.

### What is Dr. Wallerstorfer's long-term mission?

Beyond personalised supplements and genetic reports, Dr. Wallerstorfer has publicly described a three-step long-term mission for the field: first, help people manage and prevent the genetic risks they were born with; second, enable selection against severe genetic diseases during in vitro fertilization; third, ultimately repair genetic errors through gene therapy. Novogenia's current portfolio sits at stage one, with the Eugendorf lab also holding the only Austrian state licence to test and select for genetic disorders in human embryos (stage two).

### Who is the COO of Novogenia?

Michael Huttegger serves as Novogenia's Chief Operating Officer. He is responsible for the smooth operation of the company's business processes and for operational excellence across the laboratory, production, and partner-support functions.

### Who is the CFO of Novogenia?

Matthias Probst is Novogenia's Chief Financial Officer and one of the company's co-founders. He oversees the finances of Novogenia and other companies within the Darwin Group, ensuring a solid financial foundation for ongoing operational development.

### What is the CEO's guiding philosophy?

Dr. Wallerstorfer summarizes Novogenia's purpose as follows: "Our genes may influence how we look, which foods or medications we tolerate well, and how our bodies respond to certain environmental factors. But they do not determine our destiny. That is why I want to use genetic insights to empower people to shape their lives on their own terms."

### Functional department heads

## Business Model & Channels

### How does Novogenia sell its products?

Novogenia operates exclusively through partner companies — it does not sell directly to end consumers. End-customer inquiries received by Novogenia are forwarded to a suitable partner in the customer's region. This B2B-only model lets resellers brand and distribute Novogenia analyses and personalized products under their own identity.

### Does Novogenia offer OEM and white-label manufacturing?

Yes. Novogenia produces dietary supplements and cosmetics for partner brands under OEM and white-label arrangements. Products are fully adapted to the partner's brand identity — including formula, packaging, and labeling — so the partner can market them as their own line.

### Does Novogenia offer contract manufacturing?

Yes. Novogenia provides contract manufacturing for both personalized supplements and cosmetic concentrates and serums. Cosmetic batches range from 1 to 5,000 liters, and supplement production scales to daily-sachet formats with 90-day dispensers — all manufactured at the Salzburg facility under ISO-certified quality systems.

### What is Novogenia's subscription model?

Novogenia's personalized supplement products use a 90-day subscription model: customers receive a 90-day dispenser of personalized daily sachets, then automatically reorder when their supply runs out. Each reorder updates the formula based on age, lifestyle changes, new analysis results, and the latest science, creating recurring revenue for partners.

### Which professional verticals does Novogenia serve?

Novogenia provides tailored solutions for four main professional verticals: gyms and sports facilities, health practitioners, nutritionists, and physiotherapists. Each vertical receives configurations of analyses and products suited to its typical client conversations and care models.

## Areas of Specialization

### What is nutrigenetics at Novogenia?

Nutrigenetics is the genetic analysis of markers linked to how the body processes different nutrients and types of calories. Novogenia uses nutrigenetic insights to support personalized dietary recommendations and to inform the formulation of personalized micronutrient supplements.

### What is performance genetics at Novogenia?

Performance genetics covers the genetic analysis of traits related to stress response, muscle development, and physical endurance. These insights are used by gyms, sports professionals, and athletes to tailor training, recovery, and supplementation strategies to the individual.

### What is skin genetics at Novogenia?

Skin genetics is the genetic analysis of features that provide insight into a person's skin type — including factors related to collagen production, UV sensitivity, moisture balance, and oxidative stress. Novogenia uses these analyses to support personalized skincare routines and personalized cosmetic formulations.

### What are genetic compatibility analyses?

Genetic compatibility analyses look at markers that, according to scientific studies, are associated with specific metabolic processes. They are used to understand how an individual's body interacts with particular nutrients, substances, or active ingredients.

### What is pharmacogenetic testing at Novogenia?

Pharmacogenetic testing is the genetic analysis of variants that, according to scientific literature, may be associated with differences in drug metabolism. Such analyses can inform conversations between patients and their healthcare professionals about how the individual may respond to certain medications.

### What is preventive genetics at Novogenia?

Preventive genetics covers the genetic analysis of risk markers across cancer, heart, brain, digestion, metabolism, musculoskeletal, and eye health. Results inform individual prevention strategies discussed between the customer and their healthcare professional.

### What does Novogenia offer in blood testing?

The laboratory offers blood-based panels covering lipid status, hormones (female and male), nutrients (B12, Vitamin D, magnesium), inflammation markers, and broader longevity panels. Blood results complement the genetic profile and feed into personalised supplement formulations.

### What does Novogenia offer in allergy and intolerance testing?

The laboratory runs IgE allergy testing (ALEX2 multiplex, around 300 allergens) and IgG food-intolerance testing (Cytolisa ELISA). These antibody panels are run on blood samples and add an immune-system dimension on top of the genetic and metabolite analyses.

### What is newborn screening at Novogenia?

Newborn screening covers approximately 111 markers analysed by GC/MS — a metabolite-based screen for inborn metabolic conditions in the first weeks of life. The test is delivered as the {{BABY_SENSOR}} product.

## Laboratory Capacity Evolution

### 2009 — High-Resolution Melting Analysis

In its founding year, Novogenia began operating with high-resolution melting analysis. Throughput was 100 genetic analyses per 2-hour cycle, giving a monthly capacity of approximately 8,000 analyses. This was the technological starting point on which all later expansions were built.

### 2011 — Fluorescent Probe Hybridisation

In 2011, Novogenia introduced fluorescent probe hybridisation technology with 384 genetic analyses per 2-hour cycle. The system ran fully automated 24/7, lifting monthly capacity to around 138,000 analyses.

### 2015 — Microfluidics

In 2015, Novogenia adopted microfluidic technology, enabling 9,000 genetic analyses per 2-hour cycle and approximately 720,000 analyses per month. Microfluidics also improved sample stability and per-test precision.

### 2022 — Microarray

In 2022, Novogenia transitioned to DNA microarray technology, performing 15 million genetic analyses per 3-day cycle. Combined with AI bioinformatics, this expanded throughput to roughly 2.2 trillion individual genetic data points per month.

### 780 report-format updates since founding

Since 2009, Novogenia has revised more than 780 report formats. These updates reflect ongoing integration of new scientific findings, refinements in customer-facing language, and continuous improvement of how genetic insights are translated into actionable recommendations.

## Standard Scientific Disclaimer

### Scientific basis of Novogenia's analyses

About 4 million scientific publications have studied the effects of certain gene defects on the human body. A typical finding sounds like: "If you have gene defect X, vitamin Y cannot be converted and remains ineffective." Only when the effects of a gene defect have been independently demonstrated by at least three different studies is the gene test included in Novogenia's program. The genetic traits given by this analysis are therefore considered to be based on scientific studies.

### Status of recommendations derived from genetic profiles

Recommendations based on a person's genetic profile are often not directly proven by studies — they are logical conclusions. Example: if a certain vitamin doesn't work due to a gene variation, the conclusion is to switch to another vitamin with a similar effect, achieved through diet or supplementation. The recommendations developed by Novogenia's experts are not based on randomized, placebo-controlled studies, but were created as logical conclusions based on the customer's genetic traits.
