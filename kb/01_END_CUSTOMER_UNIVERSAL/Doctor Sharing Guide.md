## Why Share with a Doctor

### When sharing helps

Customers on multiple medications, customers with chronic conditions, customers planning surgery, customers with specific clinical concerns, or customers whose doctor is interested in nutrigenetics: in all these cases, sharing the {{COMPANY_NAME}} analysis and supplement formulation with the treating physician adds value.

### When sharing is not necessary

For routine personal use without medication or chronic conditions, sharing with a doctor is optional. The supplement is designed to simulate a healthy diet and no drug interactions are known. The customer can use the product as instructed without involving their physician.

## What to Share

### The genetic report

The customer-facing report (PDF or printed) contains the full genetic analysis with PubMed source citations at the end of each chapter. This is the document doctors find most useful — they can audit the science directly and form their own clinical view.

### The supplement formulation

If on {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}}, the customer's exact formulation (ingredients and dosages) is also in their account. The chatbot can guide the customer to export or print this. Sharing the formulation lets the doctor assess interaction risk against prescribed medications.

### Blood and metabolite data

If the customer has done any Abbott blood panel ({{BLOOD_PANEL_HEART_METABOLIC}}, {{BLOOD_PANEL_HORMONES_FEMALE}}, {{BLOOD_PANEL_HORMONES_MALE}}, {{BLOOD_PANEL_NUTRIENTS}}, {{BLOOD_PANEL_INFLAMMATION}}, {{BLOOD_PANEL_LONGEVITY_BASE}}, {{BLOOD_PANEL_LONGEVITY_HORMONES_FEMALE}}, {{BLOOD_PANEL_LONGEVITY_HORMONES_MALE}}), {{METABOLISM_SENSOR}}, or related blood tests, those results are particularly useful for doctors as they speak directly to the clinical vocabulary doctors are trained in.

## How to Share

### Via printed copy

If the reseller delivers a printed report, the customer can simply bring it to the appointment. This is often the most practical option — the printed book is structured to be readable by both the customer and a clinician.

### Via PDF / digital portal

The customer can download the PDF report from the {{REPORT_PORTAL}} and email it to their doctor, or print it themselves. Most modern medical practices accept PDF reports via email or patient-portal upload.

### Via clinician channel (Expert line products)

For NovoDaily Expert products (Prevention bundle, Fertility, Pregnancy, Multi Cancer Sensor), reports are sent directly to the ordering clinician. Customers in this case do not need to share separately — the doctor receives the result by default.

## What Doctors Typically Find Useful

### MTHFR status

Doctors familiar with nutrigenetics often ask first about MTHFR status because it affects folate metabolism, homocysteine, and prescription-drug interactions. The {{NUTRITION_SENSOR}} report covers this clearly.

### {{PHARMACO_SENSOR}} results

For patients on multiple drugs, the pharmacogenetic profile is directly actionable. CYP2D6, CYP2C19, CYP2C9, and other CYP variants influence dose-response for many common medications. Doctors trained in pharmacogenetics can adjust prescriptions based on this.

### Cardiovascular gene panel

For cardiology consultations, the cardiovascular and lipid-related gene findings — combined with the lipid blood panel if available — provide a richer risk assessment than blood values alone.

### Iron metabolism (HFE)

For unexplained fatigue or elevated ferritin, HFE status from {{NUTRITION_SENSOR}} flags hereditary haemochromatosis risk. This is often the moment a doctor decides to investigate iron overload more deeply.

## What Doctors May Not Be Familiar With

### Nutrigenetic personalisation principles

Some doctors are unfamiliar with the framework of "confirmed genetic traits" versus "logical-conclusion recommendations". When sharing, the customer can point the doctor to the disclaimer text at the front of the report — this typically establishes the right framing quickly.

### The {{PELLET_NAME}} technology

Doctors are used to capsules and tablets. The pellet delivery format may need a one-paragraph explanation: indigestible plant-fibre microbeads carrying nutrients, designed for controlled release. The report includes a brief explanation.

### Personalised supplement formulation logic

Doctors may want to understand how the formulation is computed before endorsing it. The {{COMPANY_NAME}} report describes the algorithm framework (genetic + blood + lifestyle inputs producing a recipe). For deeper queries, the reseller can connect the doctor with the {{COMPANY_NAME}} science team.

## What to Ask the Doctor

### Suggested customer questions to their doctor

"Are there any of my current medications that could interact with these ingredients?" "Are there specific dosages here you would adjust given my history?" "Are there any contraindications I should know about?" "Should I monitor any specific blood values while taking this supplement?"

### When the doctor pushes back

Doctors sometimes prefer their patients not to add unfamiliar supplements. The customer's choice is theirs — but the chatbot should respect medical advice and avoid encouraging the customer to take a product their doctor has specifically advised against. The reseller's support channel can help mediate where useful.
