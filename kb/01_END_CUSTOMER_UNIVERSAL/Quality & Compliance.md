## Certifications

### ISO 9001:2015 — Quality Management

Novogenia holds ISO 9001:2015 certification for general quality management. The scope covers genetic analyses, medical diagnostics, cosmetics, and dietary supplements — meaning the same quality-management system spans laboratory, production, and customer-facing operations.

### ISO 22000:2018 — Food Safety

Novogenia holds ISO 22000:2018 certification for food safety quality management, covering dietary supplement production. The standard requires documented control of every step from raw material intake to finished product shipping.

### EN ISO 22716:2007 — Cosmetics GMP

Novogenia's cosmetics production is certified to EN ISO 22716:2007 — the European Good Manufacturing Practice standard for cosmetics. The certification covers the personalised cosmetics facility in Eugendorf.

### American CLIA certification

Novogenia holds American CLIA (Clinical Laboratory Improvement Amendments) certification — the US federal standard for clinical laboratory operations. This certification is required for laboratories whose results are used in clinical or medical decision-making in the United States.

### Austrian state authorization for medical genetics

Novogenia holds a formal state authorization for medical genetics issued by the Austrian Federal Ministry of Health. This is a separate regulatory authorization on top of ISO certifications, specifically permitting genetic-analysis services within the Austrian healthcare framework.

## Laboratory Quality

### ISO 15189 standard applied across all analyses

Although Novogenia is not formally accredited to ISO 15189, the standard — which defines requirements for medical laboratory quality and competence — is applied across all analyses as the internal quality benchmark.

### In-house quality-control software

Novogenia developed proprietary software that automates quality control across the laboratory. The system tracks every sample through every analysis step, with documented checks at each stage. External auditors have consistently awarded outstanding evaluations for the QC implementation.

### Sample re-request rate

Novogenia's sample re-request rate is approximately 1 in 150 — significantly below the industry average of 1 in 30. This is achieved through a structured sampling process, tube drying, and the ability to perform up to three independent analyses from a single saliva sample.

### Sample stability

Saliva samples collected in {{COMPANY_NAME}}'s proprietary collection device remain stable for two weeks across a wide temperature range (-10°C to +45°C). This makes postal delivery worldwide reliable without specialised cold-chain logistics.

### Saliva collection device

The collection device is a sealed tube containing dried stabilisation salts. When saliva is added, the salts dissolve and immediately stabilise the DNA. Each tube carries a unique data-matrix code that lets the laboratory automation track the sample through every step without manual handling, and that decouples customer identity from the sample during analysis.

### DNA extraction

DNA is extracted from the saliva sample using a Magnetic-Bead method — DNA binds to magnetic particles which are then physically separated from contaminants. This delivers high purity and high consistency, both prerequisites for the downstream microarray analysis.

### European proficiency testing

The laboratory participates in European proficiency-testing programmes — blind-test rounds run by external bodies in which laboratories analyse the same reference material and are compared. Consistent performance in proficiency testing is one of the strongest external signals of analytical accuracy.

## Production Quality

### Made in Austria

All laboratory analysis, supplement production, and cosmetics production take place at the Eugendorf facility in Salzburg, Austria. Nothing is outsourced. The "Made in Austria" mark on finished products reflects this single-site integrated operation.

### Water purification process

Production-grade water at the facility is produced through a multi-step process: reverse osmosis, followed by double distillation, followed by storage at 80°C to eliminate bacterial growth. This is well above standard bottled-water purity and is necessary for consistent product quality.

### Doping and contaminant testing

Every batch of finished supplement product is tested for doping substances and contaminants before release. The testing covers the WADA-banned substance list as well as common contaminants. This makes the products suitable for use by competitive athletes who require clean-supplement certification.

### EU cosmetics regulation compliance

Every individual personalised cosmetic formula receives a full EU cosmetics-regulation safety dossier — typically 150+ pages — including toxicological assessment and registration. Because formulas are individual, the dossier work is fully automated, allowing this to operate at industrial scale.

### ISO 22000 supplement quality controls

Under the ISO 22000 framework, every batch of raw material is tested before use: microbiological testing, nutrient content analysis, doping-substance screening, and IR identity verification. Retention samples are stored against future reference.

### Per-batch supplement quality testing

Each nutrient batch produced in Eugendorf undergoes more than 100 individual quality tests before dispatch — covering identity, purity, potency, contaminant limits, and microbiological safety. The 100+ test protocol is what allows {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}} to claim ISO-grade purity at milligram-level dosing precision.

## Data Protection

### Strict medical data protection

Customer data is treated under strict medical data-protection standards, on top of standard GDPR requirements. Identity, address, order data, consent records, and laboratory results are stored under controlled access with documented retention periods.

### Sample handling and DNA destruction

DNA extracted from the saliva sample is destroyed after analysis — no further analyses can be performed on it. Identity is decoupled from the sample during laboratory analysis: the lab works with barcoded tubes only, and only after analysis is complete are results linked back to customer identity.

### Use of DNA data

DNA-derived data is used only for the specific purposes the customer ordered (the analyses they purchased). It is not shared for commercial purposes and is not sold to research or marketing parties.

### Customer-initiated deletion

Customers can request deletion of their data. The retention of identity and order data follows applicable legal data-retention requirements; outside those, deletion is honoured on request.
