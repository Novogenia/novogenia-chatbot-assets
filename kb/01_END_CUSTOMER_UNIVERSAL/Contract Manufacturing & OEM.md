## Overview

### What Novogenia offers as a contract manufacturer

Novogenia produces personalised dietary supplements and personalised cosmetics for partner brands under both OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) and white-label arrangements. Products are fully adapted to the partner's brand identity — formula, packaging, labelling — so the partner can market the products as their own line.

### Why partners choose Novogenia

Single integrated 4,000 m² Austrian facility combining laboratory, supplement production, and cosmetics production. Proprietary {{PELLET_NAME}} technology unavailable from other manufacturers. Full EU regulatory dossier work handled in-house and automated to industrial scale. ~85 employees today with proven elasticity to 1,500 during COVID — demonstrated ability to scale.

## Supplement Contract Manufacturing

### Personalised formulations

Personalised formulations based on the partner's customer data — genetic data, blood values, lifestyle questionnaire — or any combination the partner chooses. Custom dosing at milligram precision is the differentiator versus standard contract manufacturers who work at the SKU level.

### Production format

Products are produced in daily {{SACHET_NAME}}s and packed into 3-month dispensers, matching the standard subscription rhythm. Custom packaging formats and pack sizes are possible on request for sufficient volume.

### Quality framework

All supplement production runs under ISO 22000:2018 (food safety) and ISO 9001:2015 (general quality). Every raw material batch is tested before use: microbiological, nutrient content, doping substances, IR identity. Per-batch retention samples are stored against future reference.

### B2B integration

Partner systems can integrate via API to push customer analysis data and pull formulation status. Novogenia supports medical-platform partners (NovoMedic-style integrations) and large-reseller workflows.

## Cosmetics Contract Manufacturing

### Concentrate and serum batches

Personalised cosmetic concentrates and serums produced in batches ranging from 1 to 5,000 litres. The same automated cosmetics pipeline that runs personalised individual production also runs partner-branded batch production.

### Full registration included

Every individual formula receives a full EU cosmetics-regulation safety dossier — typically 150+ pages — including toxicological assessment, microbiological monitoring, and registration. This dossier work is fully automated, which is what enables industrial-scale personalisation.

### Production capacity

Cosmetics production capacity is being expanded to support up to €100 million in annual revenue from this segment. Partners planning at-scale roll-outs should engage early to lock in capacity.

### Personalisation depth

Over 30 billion possible cosmetic formulas based on genetic analysis of 20+ skin-related genes. Partners can pick how much personalisation depth to expose to their end customers — from generic types through to fully individual formulations.

## White-label and Private-label Services

### Brand adaptation

All products and services can be adapted to partner brand identity. The partner's logo, name, colours, copy, and packaging design are applied throughout — physical product, packaging, printed reports, digital portal, and any chatbot. The end customer experiences a fully partner-branded product.

### What stays Novogenia

The underlying laboratory analytics and the {{PELLET_NAME}} technology are Novogenia's. Manufacturing happens in Eugendorf. Where the partner needs "Made in Austria" labelling, that flows through automatically; where the partner prefers neutral country labelling, that is also possible.

### Subscription model included

The 90-day subscription reorder model is available out of the box. Partners can use Novogenia's billing infrastructure or integrate Novogenia as a fulfilment provider behind the partner's own billing.

## Engagement Path

### Initial engagement

B2B prospects engage via the partner-onboarding process. Initial scope typically covers: target product mix, target volume, target markets, integration model (full white-label vs hybrid), and timeline.

### Pilot phase

A short pilot phase is recommended for new partners — typically 200–500 customers — to validate end-to-end flow before committing to large-scale roll-out. Pilot pricing is structured to be accessible.

### Scale-up

Once the pilot is successful, scale-up to thousands or tens of thousands of monthly customers follows the partner's commercial rhythm. Capacity planning is collaborative.

### Contact

Partner-onboarding inquiries are routed through Novogenia's B2B sales channel rather than the consumer support channel. The reseller's contact will provide the appropriate route.
