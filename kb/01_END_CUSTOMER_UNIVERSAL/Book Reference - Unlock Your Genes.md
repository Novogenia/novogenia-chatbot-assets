## Chapter index

### Chapter — How It All Started

Daniel's origin story: how the field of nutrigenetics emerged, and why personalised genetic insight became the focus of his career and of {{COMPANY_NAME}}.

### Chapter — The Angelina Effect (Genes and Cancer)

How Angelina Jolie's BRCA1/BRCA2 announcement shifted public understanding of hereditary cancer. Covers BRCA, other breast-cancer-associated genes, age-related cancer risk, and the influence of Vitamin D on breast tissue. Practical takeaway for women carrying BRCA variants.

### Chapter — The Celtic Curse (When Iron Becomes Toxic)

Hereditary hemochromatosis — the HFE gene, why misdiagnosis is so common, treatment and prevention options, and the case for routine HFE genetic screening. Cost analysis of follow-up costs of misdiagnosis vs. early detection.

### Chapter — The Blood Clot (When Blood Supply is Blocked)

Factor V Leiden mutation and thrombosis prevention. Why daily aspirin advice is more nuanced than commonly understood. Special section on thrombosis prevention in pregnant women.

### Chapter — The Berlin Patient (Viruses: Genetic Poison or Ingenious Therapy)

HIV resistance via the CCR5 gene defect — the famous Berlin Patient case. Why HIV infection is not yet routinely curable, and how viruses themselves are now being repurposed as genetic tools (gene therapy vectors).

### Chapter — Dangerous Cure (How Your Genes Influence the Effect of Drugs)

Pharmacogenetics — drugs that need to be activated by the body's own enzymes, the pro-drug Codeine as the classic example, and where personalised medication is heading. Underpins the {{PHARMACO_SENSOR}} product.

### Chapter — The Story of Lactose

How the LCT gene evolved as humans began drinking milk, the different forms of lactose intolerance (primary, congenital, acquired), and cow's-milk protein allergy as a distinct condition.

### Chapter — The Story of Gluten

How an incorrectly programmed immune system leads to celiac disease. The DQ-A1 and DQ-B1 receptor genes. The diet-based intervention and why celiac disease has become more visible in recent years.

### Chapter — The Gene Diet (How Genes Influence Body Weight)

The big-picture chapter on weight: the LEP gene, the GHRL hunger gene, the FTO obesity gene, and how genes determine whether someone burns fat or carbs better. The "weighty genes" overview includes FABP2, PPARG, ADRB2, ADRB3 and others. Underpins {{WEIGHT_SENSOR}} and {{SUPPLEMENT_WEIGHT}}.

### Chapter — The Fire Brigade (Oxidative Stress)

How the body protects itself from free radicals — SOD2, GPX1, CAT, NQO1 and related genes. Why antioxidant supplementation matters more for some genotypes than others.

### Chapter — The Scourge of the Seas (Genes and Vitamin C)

The historical story of scurvy, how humans uniquely lost the ability to make Vitamin C, and how individual genetic variants change Vitamin C needs today.

### Chapter — The Food Mystery (How Gene Defects Influence Nutrient Effects)

The central insight behind the {{COMPANY_NAME}} platform — that the same food can be helpful for one person and counter-productive for another based on which genes they carry. The conceptual foundation for {{NUTRITION_SENSOR}}.

### Chapter — The Lipid Legacy (How Genes Control Blood Lipids)

LDL, HDL, triglycerides and the genes that regulate them — APOE, APOA1, LDLR, CETP. Why the same diet produces very different lipid profiles in different people. Connects to {{BLOOD_PANEL_HEART_METABOLIC}} and {{BLOOD_PANEL_LONGEVITY_BASE}}.

### Chapter — A Happy Century (Detoxification and Addiction)

How the body breaks down toxins (CYP family, GST family, NAT2), why some people tolerate alcohol or caffeine better than others, and the genetic component of addiction risk. Underpins {{TOXO_SENSOR}}.

### Chapter — The Asian Glow

The ALDH2 gene defect common in East Asian populations causing facial flushing and worse alcohol metabolism — and how this single variant has shaped culture and drinking norms.

### Chapter — Born To Be Great (Genes and Athletic Performance)

The ACTN3 "sprinter gene", ACE for endurance, and the cluster of variants determining response to training, recovery rates, and injury risk. Foundation for {{PERFORMANCE_SENSOR}}.

### Chapter — The Man With Two Faces (Genes and Skin Aging)

Why the two sides of an aged face often look so different — the genetic contribution to skin ageing, collagen breakdown, UV damage response, and the science behind {{BEAUTY_SENSOR}} and personalised cosmetics.

### Chapter — The Genetic Memory (Epigenetics and Methylation)

How lifestyle and environment turn genes on and off — the methylation cycle, why MTHFR matters so much, and the long shadow of choices made today on future health.

### Chapter — Into the Blood

Why blood tests now sit alongside genetic tests in the {{COMPANY_NAME}} platform. Bridges to {{BLOOD_PANEL_LONGEVITY_BASE}} and related panels.

### Chapter — Escape Velocity (Longevity and the Dream of Eternal Life)

The science of biological ageing, the genes that influence lifespan, and the realistic limits of life-extension as of today. Underpins {{BIOLOGICAL_AGE_SENSOR}}.

### Chapter — Instructions from Nature (Structure of Our Genome)

The reference chapter on genome architecture — DNA, chromosomes, genes, alleles, mutations — for readers who want to anchor everything else in the basics.

### Chapter — A Few Words in Conclusion

Daniel's closing thoughts on the future of precision health.
