## The "+" Subscription Concept

### What "+" means

The "+" suffix on a product name always indicates a subscription product. {{PACKAGE_LIFESTYLE_PLUS}} is the subscription version of Lifestyle. {{PACKAGE_SHAPE_PLUS}} is the subscription version of Shape. The "+" subscription bundles the matching DNA test free of charge with a recurring supplement or cosmetic supply.

### Why subscribe rather than buy one-time

One-time purchase: customer pays full price for the DNA test, then optionally pays per-order for the supplement. Subscription: the DNA test is included free, payments are spread over time as a flat monthly fee, and the supplement formulation updates automatically with each reorder.

## Billing Rhythm

### Monthly billing

All "+" subscriptions are billed monthly. The first instalment is charged at order placement. From the point of the first product delivery, monthly billing continues automatically until the customer pauses or cancels.

### 3-month delivery cycle

Supplements and cosmetics are produced and shipped in 3-month packs. The customer therefore receives a physical delivery every three months while paying monthly — three monthly instalments per delivery cycle.

### What this means in practice

For {{PACKAGE_LIFESTYLE_PLUS}}: roughly €3.80 per day × 30 days ≈ €114 per month. Three months × €114 ≈ €342 per shipment, with one shipment every three months. Total per 12-month minimum term: roughly €1,368.

## Minimum Subscription Terms

### Why minimums exist

The minimum term covers the cost of the included DNA test and the personalised report. The monthly fee structure assumes a minimum length over which the test cost is amortised.

### Term by product

{{PACKAGE_LIFESTYLE_PLUS}}: 12 months minimum. {{PACKAGE_SHAPE_PLUS}}: 12 months minimum. {{PACKAGE_BEAUTY_PLUS}}: 12 months minimum. {{PACKAGE_PERFORMANCE_PLUS}}: 15 months minimum (longer because of more elaborate formulation work). Standalone supplement/cosmetic subscriptions without a free DNA test ({{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}}+, {{SUPPLEMENT_WEIGHT}}+, {{COSMETICS_DAY_NIGHT_95ML}}+, {{COSMETICS_BODY_LOTION_235ML}}+): 3-month cycles, cancellable at the end of any cycle.

### After the minimum term

The subscription auto-renews in 3-month cycles. The customer can cancel online at any time after the minimum term, with cancellation taking effect at the end of the current 3-month cycle.

---

## Cancelling Before the Minimum Term

### What happens if the customer cancels early?

The DNA test that came free with the subscription is included free during the minimum term. Cancellation before the minimum term ends may result in the value of the DNA test becoming payable. Exact terms are stated at checkout and in the published commercial rules of the reseller.

### Practical advice for customers

If a customer is unsure about a long commitment, suggest starting with a one-time DNA test ({{PACKAGE_LIFESTYLE}} or {{PACKAGE_SHAPE}}) and adding the supplement subscription separately later as {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}}+ or {{SUPPLEMENT_WEIGHT}}+. This avoids the early-cancellation fee scenario.

---

## Pausing a Subscription

### How pausing works

The customer can pause at any time online. While paused, no further shipments are produced and no further monthly billing occurs.

### Effect on the minimum term

Pause time does not count toward the minimum subscription term. Example: 6 months active, 2 months paused, 6 months active = 12 months active (minimum met).

### Resuming

The customer can resume at any time. The next shipment uses the latest formulation incorporating any algorithm improvements made during the pause period.

---

## Reformulation Per Reorder

### What changes per reorder

The supplement formulation is recalculated for every 3-month shipment. Inputs that may change: customer's age (older customers may need different dosing), any new analyses added since the last shipment, any updated lifestyle questionnaire responses, new science integrated into the algorithm.

### What does not change

The customer's genetic results do not change between reorders. The same gene variants are re-used; only the interpretation and the resulting dosing may evolve.

### Frequency of algorithm improvement

The formulation algorithm is improved approximately once per week on average across all customers. Individual customers see these improvements at their next reorder rather than mid-cycle.

## Adding to a Subscription

### Adding more analyses

A customer can add further analyses to an active subscription — for example adding {{ALLERGY_SENSOR}}, {{METABOLISM_SENSOR}}, or any Abbott blood panel ({{BLOOD_PANEL_HEART_METABOLIC}}, {{BLOOD_PANEL_NUTRIENTS}}, {{BLOOD_PANEL_LONGEVITY_BASE}}, etc.) to an existing {{PACKAGE_LIFESTYLE_PLUS}}. New analyses are billed separately and their results automatically feed into the next supplement reformulation.

### Adding cosmetics

A customer on {{PACKAGE_LIFESTYLE_PLUS}} can add {{COSMETICS_DAY_NIGHT_95ML}} or {{COSMETICS_BODY_LOTION_235ML}} as separate subscriptions running in parallel. Each subscription has its own 3-month cycle.

### Switching products

Customers can switch from one "+" subscription to another (e.g. {{PACKAGE_LIFESTYLE_PLUS}} to {{PACKAGE_PERFORMANCE_PLUS}}). The free DNA tests already received remain valid; additional tests required for the new product are added. The reseller's support channel handles the switch mechanics.
