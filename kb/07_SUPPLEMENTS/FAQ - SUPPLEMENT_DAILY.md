## Taking {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}}

### How is {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}} taken?

The recommended way is to stir the {{PELLET_NAME}} into yogurt and eat them with breakfast. Alternatively, swallow them with small sips of water. Always take with a meal. Do not chew the {{PELLET_NAME}} on purpose — the time-delayed release relies on the intact bead structure. Accidental chewing is not harmful but reduces the slow-release benefit. Discontinue one week before a scheduled colonoscopy to allow the intestine to clear.

### How is the dose of each nutrient set?

Each personalised dose sits within a defined window: a minimum of about 15% of the recommended daily allowance (RDA) for any included nutrient, up to roughly 300% of the RDA where the customer's genetics or blood values justify a higher dose. The exact dose is set by the algorithm — never below the minimum that delivers an effect, never above EFSA upper safe limits.

### I have difficulty swallowing the {{PELLET_NAME}}. What can I do?

Either take them in small amounts with plenty of liquid, or stir them evenly into yogurt. When mixed into yogurt, they do not dissolve immediately and can be consumed as normal.

### Can {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}} be taken daily and long-term?

Yes. {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}} is specifically designed for daily, long-term use. Even combined with a nutrient-rich diet and taken every day, total intake never reaches the upper safe limit for any nutrient as defined by the European Food Safety Authority (EFSA). The dosages accumulate to neither unhealthy nor excessive levels in the body.

### Can I take {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}} during pregnancy?

During pregnancy, the priority shifts to the health and development of the embryo. Standardized prenatal supplements formulated to support embryo growth are recommended — not a personalized supplement optimized for the mother's genetics. Stop {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}} during pregnancy and follow your doctor's recommendation for a suitable prenatal supplement.

### Can I combine {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}} with other nutritional supplements?

Yes, other supplements can generally be taken alongside {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}}. The important rule is to avoid supplements containing the same active ingredients — if the same nutrient is coming from two sources, the total dose exceeds what was calculated for you, which throws off the personalization. As long as there is no ingredient overlap, adding other supplements is fine.

### When should I take {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}}?

Always take {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}} with a meal — breakfast is recommended. If you also use {{SUPPLEMENT_WEIGHT}}, take that separately before lunch and dinner, never combined: the calorie blocker can absorb fat-soluble vitamins from the daily supplement. If you receive two {{SACHET_NAME}}s per day, take them together at breakfast or spread them across the day.

### I take many medications. Is taking {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}} advisable?

{{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}} is designed to simulate the effect of a healthy, optimized diet — it contains the same nutrients found in food, just personalized. Drug interactions are not known and have not been reported. If you have specific concerns, share your supplement formulation with your doctor; the report includes the exact ingredients and dosages.

This is very uncommon. Only natural raw materials are used — the same forms found in healthy foods. No cases are known in which vitamins and minerals from {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}} caused negative health effects. If persistent issues occur, a doctor should be consulted.

The effects discussed in scientific literature refer to very high single doses (e.g., 1,000 mg Vitamin C at once). {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}} releases a maximum of 300 mg of Vitamin C slowly over 12 hours — approximately 40 times less than the performance-impairing dose. Where possible and meaningful, micronutrients have been switched to highly bioavailable forms. For example, Vitamin B12 is provided as active methylcobalamin. Folic acid is provided as folic acid or active methylfolate depending on the customer's MTHFR gene result.
