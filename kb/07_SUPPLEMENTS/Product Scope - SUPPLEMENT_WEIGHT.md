## Overview

### What is {{SUPPLEMENT_WEIGHT}}?

{{SUPPLEMENT_WEIGHT}} is a personalised calorie blocker in {{PELLET_NAME}} form. It contains an individually calibrated ratio of two active plant-based ingredients — a fat blocker and a carbohydrate blocker — with the ratio determined by the customer's genetic fat/carbohydrate sensitivity revealed by {{WEIGHT_SENSOR}}.

### Who is {{SUPPLEMENT_WEIGHT}} for?

Customers actively pursuing weight loss who want a supplement aligned to their genetic fat-vs-carb sensitivity, and customers managing weight maintenance after an initial loss phase. Fully plant-based — suitable for vegetarians and vegans.

## What it contains

### Active ingredients

Opuntia ficus-indica (OFI) cactus fibre as the fat blocker; Phaseolamin from white beans as the carbohydrate blocker. Both ingredients are plant-based. Lactose-free, gluten-free, free of artificial colorants and fillers.

### How it works

Taken before lunch and dinner, the {{PELLET_NAME}} interact with food in the gastrointestinal tract. OFI fibre forms a gel matrix that binds dietary fat (reducing absorption by approximately 26% in clinical evidence). Phaseolamin inhibits alpha-amylase, reducing carbohydrate breakdown and absorption.

## Deliverables

### What the customer receives

A 3-month supply of {{SUPPLEMENT_WEIGHT}} {{PELLET_NAME}}, billed monthly in the standard subscription rhythm. Personalisation is calibrated from the customer's {{WEIGHT_SENSOR}} results.

### How to take it

Swallow whole — do not chew — before lunch and dinner with a large sip of water. Allow at least 4 hours between {{SUPPLEMENT_WEIGHT}} and {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}} because the calorie blocker can interfere with fat-soluble vitamin absorption from the daily supplement.
