## Overview

### What is {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}}?

{{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}} is a personalised nutritional supplement in {{PELLET_NAME}} form, containing over 20 micronutrients individually dosed and combined for each customer. It is produced fresh per customer order at Novogenia's facility in Eugendorf, Salzburg, Austria, and delivered in daily {{SACHET_NAME}}s.

### Who is {{SUPPLEMENT_DAILY}} for?

People who want their supplementation to match their actual genetics, blood values, and lifestyle — rather than the one-size-fits-all approach of standard off-the-shelf products. Particularly valuable for customers already taking multiple separate supplements who want to consolidate into a single optimised daily dose.

## What it contains

### Inputs feeding the formulation

{{NUTRITION_SENSOR}}, {{TOXO_SENSOR}}, {{BURNOUT_SENSOR}}, {{PERFORMANCE_SENSOR}}, {{BEAUTY_SENSOR}}, blood/urine analyses ({{MICRONUTRIENT_SENSOR}}, {{MICROBIOME_SENSOR}}), and the lifestyle questionnaire. The customer's available inputs are integrated by an algorithm of 200,000+ lines of code.

### Dosing precision

Each nutrient is dosed to milligram precision per individual recipe — a degree of personalisation not possible with capsules or tablets. EFSA upper safe limits are enforced as hard constraints by the algorithm.

## Deliverables

### What the customer receives

A 3-month dispenser containing 90 daily {{SACHET_NAME}}s, each holding the customer's personalised {{PELLET_NAME}} mix. Customers with higher-need profiles receive two sachets per day. Labelling shows the customer's name and recipe details.

### Reformulation per reorder

Every 3-month reorder is recalculated with the customer's current age, any new analysis data, and any new science integrated since the last reorder. Genetic data does not change; the interpretation does.
